## Solutions
**Q1 — (B).** Charles Babbage designed the Difference Engine and the Analytical Engine, the first concept of a general-purpose programmable computer. *Also remember:* Ada Lovelace, who wrote an algorithm for the Analytical Engine, is called the first computer programmer; Alan Turing is the father of theoretical computer science/AI.

**Q2 — (A).** RAM loses its data when power goes off; ROM, hard disks and flash drives are non-volatile. *Also remember:* BIOS/firmware is stored in ROM (today usually flash-based EEPROM).

**Q3 — (D).** 1st = vacuum tubes, 2nd = transistors, 3rd = ICs, 4th = microprocessors (VLSI). *Also remember:* 5th generation is associated with Artificial Intelligence and ULSI/parallel processing.

**Q4 — (C).** SWAYAM was launched in July 2017 (9 July 2017); the 2021 UGC regulations raised the credit-transfer ceiling from 20% to 40% per semester. Statement 3 is wrong — SWAYAM covers school to PG, across all disciplines. *Also remember:* SWAYAM = Study Webs of Active-Learning for Young Aspiring Minds; its sister platform SWAYAM Prabha delivers content via DTH channels.

**Q5 — (A).** Volatility *means* loss of data without power, so R correctly explains A. *Also remember:* RAM types — DRAM (needs refreshing, used as main memory) and SRAM (faster, used for cache).

**Q6 — (C).** Shodhganga – INFLIBNET (Gandhinagar); NDLI – IIT Kharagpur; DIKSHA – NCERT; NPTEL – IITs + IISc. *Also remember:* NPTEL began with seven IITs (Bombay, Delhi, Guwahati, **Kanpur**, Kharagpur, Madras, Roorkee) and IISc Bengaluru — IIT Kanpur is the UP link.

**Q7 — (B).** ENIAC (1946) → PARAM 8000 (1991) → NPTEL (2003) → SWAYAM (2017). *Also remember:* PARAM was developed by C-DAC, Pune, led by Vijay Bhatkar.

**Q8 — (D).** BIOS (Basic Input/Output System) is firmware/system-level software, not application software. *Also remember:* Application software examples — MS Word, Excel, Tally, web browsers.

**Q9 — (A).** Shodhganga is the reservoir of Indian theses; UGC's 2009 M.Phil./Ph.D. regulations required e-copies of theses to be deposited with INFLIBNET. *Also remember:* Shodhgangotri = synopses/research proposals; e-ShodhSindhu = e-journal consortium; Vidwan = expert database.

**Q10 — (D).** All three are correct. *Also remember:* IT Act, 2000 came into force on 17 October 2000; the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is India's data-protection law; complaints can be filed at cybercrime.gov.in.

**Q11 — (B).** SWAYAM Prabha uses DTH (satellite TV) and so needs no internet bandwidth; offline e-content reduces data cost. Options A, C, D either ignore affordability or abandon ICT. *Also remember:* The digital divide is the gap between those who have access to ICT (devices, connectivity, skills) and those who do not.

**Q12 — (C).** Cache is a small, very fast memory (SRAM) close to/inside the CPU — not part of the hard disk. *Also remember:* Memory speed order (fastest first): Registers > Cache > RAM > SSD/Hard disk.
