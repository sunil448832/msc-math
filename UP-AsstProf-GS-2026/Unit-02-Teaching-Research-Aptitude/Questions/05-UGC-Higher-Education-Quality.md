## Solutions
**Q1 — (B).** The Radhakrishnan Commission (1948–49) recommended a UGC modelled on the UK's University Grants Committee. *Also remember:* Maulana Abul Kalam Azad inaugurated UGC on 28 December 1953. Its first chairman was Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar.

**Q2 — (C).** UGC Act 1956 → NAAC 1994 → SWAYAM 2017 (launched 9 July 2017) → NEP 2020 (approved 29 July 2020). *Also remember:* the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) was launched on 29 July 2021, the first anniversary of NEP 2020.

**Q3 — (A).** 2(f) defines "University", 3 covers deemed universities, 12B governs grant eligibility, and 22 deals with the right to confer degrees. *Also remember:* Section 23 bars an institution that is not a university from using the word "University" in its name. Section 26 empowers UGC to make regulations.

**Q4 — (D).** The GER target is **50% by 2035**, not 2030. *Also remember:* NEP 2020 also proposes a 4-year multidisciplinary UG with multiple entry and exit, MERUs, the National Research Foundation, phasing out the affiliation system, and public spending on education of 6% of GDP.

**Q5 — (B).** The GEC (General Education Council) sets standards and frames graduate attributes. Funding is HEGC's job. *Also remember:* the four verticals are NHERC (regulation), NAC (accreditation), HEGC (grants) and GEC (standard setting).

**Q6 — (C).** UGC set up NAAC in 1994, with its headquarters in Bengaluru. *Also remember:* NAAC uses a 7-point grading ladder (A++ to D). Its proposed reform to Binary Accreditation followed by Maturity-Based Graded Levels (Levels 1–5) had not been fully rolled out as of 2026.

**Q7 — (A).** The 2021 SWAYAM regulations raised the ceiling to 40% per semester (the 2016 rules allowed 20%), which makes credit transfer possible. R explains A. *Also remember:* SWAYAM has four quadrants: e-tutorial, e-content, self-assessment and a discussion forum. Its national coordinators include UGC, CEC, NPTEL, AICTE and IGNOU.

**Q8 — (B).** The VBSA Bill (formerly the HECI Bill) was introduced in the Lok Sabha on 15 December 2025 and referred to a 31-member Joint Committee of Parliament. As of October 2026 it had not been enacted. *Also remember:* option (D) mixes it up with the UGC equity regulations, which the Supreme Court stayed in January 2026.

**Q9 — (D).** A college must be listed under 2(f) and declared fit under 12B to receive central (UGC) grants. *Also remember:* NIRF ranking and NBA accreditation are quality signals, not conditions for grant eligibility.

**Q10 — (C).** The 2026 equity regulations were notified on 13 January 2026 and stayed by the Supreme Court on 29 January 2026, which kept the 2012 regulations operational (as of November 2026). *Also remember:* the Centre has since told the Court that the regulations are being reconsidered.

**Q11 — (D).** NAAC is an autonomous body set up by UGC and registered as a society. It is not created by an Act of Parliament. *Also remember:* AICTE became statutory in 1987, NCTE in 1993, and the Bar Council of India under the Advocates Act, 1961. The National Medical Commission (NMC Act, 2019) replaced the MCI.

**Q12 — (A).** NIRF's five parameters are Teaching, Learning & Resources (30%), Research & Professional Practice (30%), Graduation Outcomes (20%), Outreach & Inclusivity (10%) and Perception (10%). These are the weights for the Overall category. *Also remember:* NIRF was launched by the then MHRD in 2015, with the first rankings in 2016. In NIRF 2025, IIT Madras topped the Overall category for the 7th year running.
