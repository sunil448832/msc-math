## Solutions
**Q1 — (A).** An enumerator fills in a schedule, while respondents fill in a questionnaire themselves (often by mail or online). *Also remember:* schedules work for illiterate respondents, as in Census enumeration. Questionnaires are cheaper but have lower response rates.

**Q2 — (B).** Roll numbers only label (nominal), ranks give order (ordinal), °C has no true zero (interval), and income has a true zero (ratio). *Also remember:* the permissible averages are mode for nominal, median for ordinal, and mean for interval and ratio.

**Q3 — (C).** Quota sampling is non-probability sampling (the interviewer chooses within quotas). *Also remember:* the probability methods are simple random, systematic, stratified, cluster and multistage. The non-probability methods are convenience, purposive/judgemental, quota and snowball.

**Q4 — (D).** Groups that are homogeneous within, with random selection from each in proportion to size, make proportionate stratified random sampling. *Also remember:* in cluster sampling, whole natural groups (such as entire colleges) are selected at random.

**Q5 — (A).** Total = 5 × 20 = 100. New total = 100 − 30 + 10 = 80. New mean = 80 ÷ 5 = 16. *Also remember:* shortcut: change in mean = (change in one value) ÷ n = −20 ÷ 5 = −4.

**Q6 — (B).** Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean = 126 − 90 = 36. *Also remember:* since mean > median > mode here, the distribution is positively skewed.

**Q7 — (A).** Labels such as "Hindu" or "Muslim", or "male" or "female", cannot be added or averaged, so only the mode makes sense. R explains A. *Also remember:* the chi-square test is the typical test for nominal (frequency) data.

**Q8 — (D).** The social distance scale is by Bogardus (1925). Guttman developed the cumulative (scalogram) scale. *Also remember:* the Likert scale (1932) is the most common 5-point "strongly agree – strongly disagree" format.

**Q9 — (C).** 45% × 360° = 162°. *Also remember:* 1% = 3.6°, so 25% = 90° and 50% = 180°.

**Q10 — (D).** The increases are 2022: 200/800 = 25%, 2023: 200/1000 = 20%, 2024: 360/1200 = 30%. *Also remember:* the same absolute increase gives a smaller % on a bigger base, as 2022 and 2023 show (+200 each).

**Q11 — (C).** Data collected by someone else and published (NSS, Census, RBI bulletins) is secondary for the user. *Also remember:* the same Census data is primary for the Census organisation and secondary for a researcher who uses it later.

**Q12 — (B).** The correlation coefficient always lies between −1 and +1. *Also remember:* in a negatively skewed distribution, mean < median < mode. In a symmetric distribution, mean = median = mode.
