# Memory Sheet · Unit 2 · Topic 05 — Role of UGC in Qualitative Development of Higher Education
> Questions: [../Questions/05-UGC-Higher-Education-Quality.md](../Questions/05-UGC-Higher-Education-Quality.md)
> Time-sensitive facts are stated as of **November 2026**.

## 1. Core facts

### UGC: origin and Act
| Point | Fact |
|---|---|
| Recommended by | University Education Commission 1948–49 (**S. Radhakrishnan**), on the UK model |
| Inaugurated | **28 Dec 1953** by Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (Education Minister) |
| Statutory status | **UGC Act, 1956** (Act of Parliament) |
| First chairman | Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar |
| HQ | New Delhi (Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg) |
| Composition | Chairman, Vice-Chairman and 10 other members |
| Ministry | Ministry of Education (Department of Higher Education) |

### Key sections of the UGC Act
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| 2(f) | Definition of "University" (also the list of colleges under 2(f)) |
| 3 | Deemed to be university |
| 12 | Functions of UGC (coordinate and determine standards) |
| 12B | Fitness to receive central grants |
| 22 | Right to confer degrees |
| 23 | Ban on the misuse of the word "University" |
| 26 | Power to make regulations |

### Functions
Coordinating and maintaining standards of teaching, examination and research · disbursing grants · advising the Centre and the states · framing regulations on minimum standards (teacher qualifications, PhD, plagiarism, ODL/online, foreign campuses) · running UGC-NET (conducted by NTA since 2018) · Inter-University Centres (INFLIBNET Gandhinagar, IUAC New Delhi, IUCAA Pune, CEC New Delhi, NAAC Bengaluru).

### Important UGC regulations (stable facts)
| Regulation | Key point |
|---|---|
| Minimum Qualifications for Teachers, 2018 | NET/SET/SLET or PhD for Assistant Professor; API/academic performance |
| Plagiarism, 2018 | Levels 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 = up to 10 / 10–40 / 40–60 / above 60% |
| PhD Regulations, 2022 | **M.Phil discontinued**; a 4-year UG with at least 75% marks (or equivalent grade) is eligible for PhD |
| SWAYAM credit framework, 2021 | Up to **40%** of courses per semester via SWAYAM (it was 20% in 2016) |
| ABC Regulations, 2021 | Academic Bank of Credits; ABC launched **29 July 2021** |
| Foreign HEI campuses, 2023 | Foreign universities may set up campuses in India |
| Promotion of Equity in HEIs, **2026** | Notified **13 Jan 2026**, **stayed by the Supreme Court on 29 Jan 2026**; the 2012 regulations continue (Centre told the Court they are being reconsidered) |

### Quality-assurance ecosystem
| Body | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| NAAC | 1994, Bengaluru | Autonomous body of UGC (not statutory); 7-point CGPA, A++ to D. Binary plus MBGL (Levels 1–5) reform announced but not fully rolled out (2026) |
| NBA | 1994 (with AICTE); independent since 2010 | Accredits **programmes** (technical) |
| NIRF | Launched 2015, first rankings 2016 | TLR 30 · RP 30 · GO 20 · OI 10 · Perception 10. IIT Madras was No. 1 Overall in NIRF 2025, 7th year running |
| SWAYAM | 9 July 2017 | MOOCs; 4 quadrants |
| ABC | 29 July 2021 | Digital credit bank; supports multiple entry and exit |

### NEP 2020 (approved 29 July 2020) for higher education
- GER **50% by 2035**; multidisciplinary universities and colleges; MERUs; phasing out affiliation within 15 years; 4-year UG with exits (certificate after 1 year, diploma after 2, degree after 3, honours/research after 4); National Research Foundation; 6% of GDP for education.
- **HECI** is a single regulator (excluding medical and legal education) with 4 verticals:

| Vertical | Job |
|---|---|
| NHERC — National Higher Education Regulatory Council | Regulation |
| NAC — National Accreditation Council | Accreditation |
| HEGC — Higher Education Grants Council | Funding |
| GEC — General Education Council | Standard setting / learning outcomes |

### HECI becomes the VBSA Bill (as of Nov 2026)
Cabinet nod in December 2025. The **Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan Bill, 2025** was introduced in the Lok Sabha on **15 Dec 2025** and seeks to replace **UGC, AICTE and NCTE**. It was referred to a **31-member Joint Committee of Parliament** (chair: D. Purandeswari) and is **not yet enacted**.

### Other statutory/regulatory bodies
AICTE (statutory 1987) · NCTE (statutory 1993) · Bar Council of India (Advocates Act 1961) · NMC (NMC Act 2019, replaced MCI) · Pharmacy Council (1948) · Distance education: now under UGC (DEB).

## 2. Memory hooks
1. **UGC is the "headmaster plus treasurer" of universities.** It sets the standards (headmaster) and releases the money (treasurer). NAAC is the inspector, NIRF the ranking board, SWAYAM the free online classroom, ABC the bank passbook of credits.
2. **Sections "2-3-12-22" as a story.** First define who is a university (2f). Then allow some to be "deemed" (3). Give money only to the fit ones (12B). Let universities award degrees (22).
3. **Dates "53-56-94-17-20-21"**: UGC inaugurated (1953) → Act (1956) → NAAC (1994) → SWAYAM (2017) → NEP (2020) → ABC (2021). A NEP birthday trick: NEP on **29 July** 2020 and ABC on **29 July** 2021, its first birthday gift.
4. **HECI verticals "R-A-F-S"**, "Raf's" four rooms: **R**egulation (NHERC), **A**ccreditation (NAC), **F**unding (HEGC), **S**tandards (GEC). G for Grants (HEGC) and G for General (GEC) are the trap. Remember **Grants Council gives money, General Council gives standards.**
5. **NIRF weights "3-3-2-1-1"**: TLR 30, RP 30, GO 20, OI 10, PR 10. Think of a cricket score of 3-3-2-1-1 = 10 overs.
6. **NEP's GER target is "50 by 35"**: half of the youth in college by 2035.
7. **SWAYAM 20 → 40**: the credit limit doubled from 2016 to 2021.
8. **NAAC is in Bengaluru, INFLIBNET in Gandhinagar.** "Accreditation in the IT city, the library in Gandhi's city."
9. **VBSA "replaces the 3 big letters": U-A-N**, which is UGC, AICTE and NCTE. It is still in the Joint Committee "classroom" and has not graduated into law.
10. **Plagiarism bands "10-40-60"**, three cut-offs and four levels (0–3).

## 3. Don't mix up
| This | Not this |
|---|---|
| Radhakrishnan Commission 1948–49 (universities, recommended UGC) | Kothari Commission 1964–66 (national education system) |
| UGC inaugurated 1953 | UGC Act 1956 |
| NAAC — accredits **institutions** | NBA — accredits **programmes** |
| NAAC — autonomous body under UGC | AICTE/NCTE — statutory bodies |
| HEGC — grants | GEC — standards and outcomes |
| NIRF — ranking (Ministry of Education) | NAAC — accreditation (grading) |
| UGC Equity Regulations 2026 — stayed by SC | VBSA Bill 2025 — pending with the Joint Committee |
| GER target 50% by **2035** | NEP's 6% of GDP for education |

## 4. Exam traps
- **GER 50% by 2030** is wrong. The target is **2035**.
- HECI/VBSA does **not** cover medical and legal education under the NEP design.
- NAAC is **not** a statutory body.
- As of November 2026 the VBSA Bill is **not** law, and UGC still functions.
- **UGC chairperson:** the post has been held on additional charge or in an acting capacity, and reports differ. Check the latest notification before the exam rather than relying on this sheet.
- For a college seeking UGC grants, the answer is **2(f) plus 12B**, not NIRF or NAAC.
