## Solutions
**Q1 — (C).** The Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram. It does **not** pass through Odisha. *Also remember:* the Tropic of Cancer does not pass through Uttar Pradesh or Bihar.

**Q2 — (A).** The 82°30′ E meridian passes near Mirzapur and sets Indian Standard Time (IST = GMT + 5:30). *Also remember:* the meridian passes through five states: UP, MP, Chhattisgarh, Odisha and Andhra Pradesh.

**Q3 — (B).** Devprayag: Bhagirathi (the Ganga is formed here). Rudraprayag: Mandakini. Karnaprayag: Pindar. Nandprayag: Nandakini. *Also remember:* Vishnuprayag, the first of the five going downstream, is where the Dhauliganga joins the Alaknanda.

**Q4 — (D).** The Godavari flows east into the Bay of Bengal. It is the longest river of peninsular India ("Dakshin Ganga"). *Also remember:* the Narmada and Tapi flow west through rift valleys and form estuaries rather than deltas.

**Q5 — (A).** Both statements are true, and R explains A: the south-west monsoon winds blowing from the Arabian Sea are blocked by the Western Ghats, and the retreating/north-east monsoon brings Bay of Bengal moisture to the Coromandel coast. *Also remember:* Mawsynram and Cherrapunji (Meghalaya) receive the highest rainfall, from funnel-shaped valleys in the Khasi Hills on the windward side.

**Q6 — (B).** Alluvial soil covers the largest area (about 40% of the land area): the Indo-Gangetic plains, including almost all of UP. *Also remember:* black (regur) soil comes from Deccan basalt and is ideal for cotton. Laterite soil forms under heavy rainfall through leaching.

**Q7 — (C).** Dudhwa: UP (Lakhimpur Kheri). Kaziranga: Assam (one-horned rhinoceros). Gir: Gujarat (Asiatic lion). Silent Valley: Kerala (lion-tailed macaque). *Also remember:* India's first national park was Hailey NP (1936), now Jim Corbett NP in Uttarakhand.

**Q8 — (B).** UP borders 8 states (Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Bihar), plus the NCT of Delhi and Nepal internationally. *Also remember:* UP has 75 districts and 18 divisions, and is the most populous state (Census 2011).

**Q9 — (D).** All three are correctly matched. *Also remember:* Kolar Gold Fields stopped mining in 2001. Hutti (Karnataka) is now the main working gold mine. The Singrauli coalfield spans Sonbhadra (UP) and Singrauli (MP).

**Q10 — (A).** Rajasthan > Madhya Pradesh > Maharashtra > Uttar Pradesh. *Also remember:* Goa is the smallest state by area, and Sikkim the least populous.

**Q11 — (C).** Anamudi (about 2,695 m) in the Anaimalai Hills of Kerala is the highest peak of peninsular India. *Also remember:* Doddabetta is the highest point of the Nilgiris, and Guru Shikhar (Mount Abu) is the highest point of the Aravallis.

**Q12 — (D).** Ranipur Tiger Reserve (Chitrakoot district, Bundelkhand) was notified in 2022 as UP's fourth tiger reserve. *Also remember:* UP's other tiger reserves are Dudhwa (Lakhimpur Kheri), Pilibhit and Amangarh (Bijnor, the buffer of Corbett).
