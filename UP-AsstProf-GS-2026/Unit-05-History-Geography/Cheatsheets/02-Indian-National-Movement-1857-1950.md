## 3. Memory hooks

1. **The "5–7–9–11–16" staircase (number trick).** **05** Cut Bengal → **07** Split at Surat → **09** Separate electorates → **11** Undo the partition → **16** Unite at Lucknow. Read it as: "**C**ut, **S**plit, **S**eparate, **U**ndo, **U**nite", or "**C**ongress **S**plit **S**o **U**nity **U**rgent".
2. **Gandhi's "1-7-8-8 runway".** Champaran **17**, Ahmedabad **18**, Kheda **18**. "**C-A-K**e": **C**hamparan → **A**hmedabad → **K**heda, each eaten by 1918.
3. **The "even-year" Gandhian cycle.** NCM **1920** → CDM **1930** → Quit India **1942**. Roughly "every 10–12 years Gandhi launched a mass wave". Each ended or paused: Chauri Chaura ('22), Gandhi–Irwin Pact ('31), and freedom ('47).
4. **The "Si-Ne-La-Da" chain, 1928–30.** **Si**mon → **Ne**hru Report → **La**hore → **Da**ndi. Say it like a song: "**See Nehru Last Dance**." It answers the most common chronology question.
5. **RTCs: "1 2 3 = Boycott, Gandhi, Boycott".** The Congress was absent from the 1st and the 3rd; Gandhi went only to the **2nd** (1931). Ambedkar went to all three, "the 100% attendance student".
6. **Communal Award → Poona Pact, the "A before P" rule.** **A**ugust 1932 (**A**ward) → Gandhi's fast in Yerwada → **P**oona Pact, September 1932. A comes before P, just as in the alphabet.
7. **1942: "C before Q".** **C**ripps (March) came first, then **Q**uit India (August). The alphabet again: C before Q. The INA came in '43 ("**4**2 Quit, **4**3 Azad").
8. **UP map of 1857 (story link).** Travel down the Ganga by boat: **Meerut** lights the match → **Bareilly**'s **Khan** guards Rohilkhand → **Lucknow**'s **Begum** holds the Residency → **Faizabad**'s **Maulvi** preaches → **Kanpur**'s **Nana** fights at the ghats → **Allahabad**'s **Liaqat** waits at the Sangam → **Jhansi**'s **Rani** rides south. "**B**egum, **N**ana, **K**han, **M**aulvi" = "**Be Not Khan, Maulvi**" for Lucknow, Kanpur, Bareilly, Faizabad.
9. **Kakori = "the train robbery that became a trial".** Date trick: **9 August 1925**. The same "9 August" later became Quit India Day (the arrests of 1942), so August 9 is UP's revolutionary date twice. The four martyrs were hanged in four UP jails ("**G**orakhpur, **F**aizabad, **N**aini, **G**onda": **G-F-N-G**, "Good Friends Never Give up").
10. **Chauri Chaura analogy.** Gandhi as a **cricket captain who declares the innings** because his own team started a fight in the stands. He stopped the NCM to protect the principle of non-violence.
11. **Constituent Assembly countdown: "9-11-13".** **9** December first sitting (Sinha), **11** December Prasad, **13** December Objectives Resolution: odd numbers rising by 2. Then **26 November 1949** adopted, **26 January 1950** enforced: "**26 → 26**, two months apart".
12. **Viceroy relay ("C-C-I-L").** **C**urzon (cut Bengal) → **C**helmsford (Chelmsford's Jallianwala) → **I**rwin (Simon, salt) → **L**inlithgow (Quit). "**C**ongress **C**alls **I**ndia **L**oudly."
13. **Women presidents: "Annie first, Sarojini Indian first".** Besant 1917 Calcutta → Naidu 1925 **Kanpur** (UP). "Kanpur crowned the first Indian woman."

## 4. Don't mix up
| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| Lucknow 1899 vs 1916 vs 1936 | 1899 R.C. Dutt; 1916 Pact (Mazumdar); 1936 Nehru |
| Lahore 1929 (INC) vs Lahore 1940 (League) | Purna Swaraj vs the Pakistan Resolution |
| Lucknow Pact (1916) vs Poona Pact (1932) | INC–League vs Gandhi–Ambedkar |
| Gandhi–Irwin Pact (1931) vs Poona Pact (1932) | Ended the first phase of CDM vs depressed-class seats |
| Cripps Mission (1942) vs Cabinet Mission (1946) | Cripps was a member of both. 1942 = war-time offer; 1946 = plan for the Constituent Assembly |
| HRA (1924) vs HSRA (1928) | HRA: Bismil and Sachindra Nath Sanyal (Kakori). HSRA: added "Socialist" (Bhagat Singh, Azad) |
| Sachchidananda Sinha vs Rajendra Prasad | Provisional vs permanent President of the CA |
| Partition 1905 vs Annulment 1911 | Curzon vs Hardinge II |
| Mohan Singh vs Subhas Bose (INA) | Mohan Singh first organised the INA (1942); Bose took over in 1943 |
| 26 January 1930 vs 26 January 1950 | Purna Swaraj Day vs Republic Day (chosen because of 1930) |

## 5. Exam traps
- Gandhi presided over the INC **only once** (Belgaum 1924).
- The **Chauri Chaura** violence happened in February 1922, but the NCM was **withdrawn** at Bardoli (Congress Working Committee, 12 February 1922).
- **Chandrashekhar Azad was NOT hanged.** He died in Alfred Park (now Chandrashekhar Azad Park), Allahabad, on 27 February 1931.
- The Simon Commission was **appointed in November 1927** and arrived in **February 1928**. If the options say 1927 or 1928, check whether the question says "appointed" or "arrived".
- The Indian Independence Act **abolished the Secretary of State**, not the Governor-General.
- **First** woman INC president: Annie Besant (1917). **First Indian** woman: Sarojini Naidu (1925, Kanpur).
- The Constituent Assembly was formed under the **Cabinet Mission Plan**, not the Cripps Mission.
- The 1857 revolt started at **Meerut** on 10 May. Mangal Pandey's protest (**Barrackpore**, 29 March) came earlier but is not counted as the start.
