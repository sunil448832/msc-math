### UNESCO World Heritage: India (as of November 2026)
- **Total: 45**, made up of 37 cultural, 7 natural and 1 mixed (Khangchendzonga NP).
- **Newest:** Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath, inscribed on **25 July 2026** at the **48th session, Busan (South Korea)**. It is India's 45th site and **UP's 4th**.
- The 44th was the Maratha Military Landscapes (2025, 47th session, Paris).
- The **first sites (1983)** were Ajanta, Ellora, Agra Fort and the Taj Mahal.
- **UP's four:** Taj Mahal (1983), Agra Fort (1983), Fatehpur Sikri (1986) and Sarnath (2026).

## 2. Memory hooks

1. **Harappan sites as a city tour (analogy).** **Lothal** is the *port* ("Lot of boats"). **Kalibangan** is the *farm* ("Kali" = black bangles of a farmer's wife ploughing). **Mohenjo-daro** is the *swimming pool* (Great Bath). **Dholavira** is the *billboard and water tank*.
2. **"Alam-GIR-pur = the sun sets over the east of Harappan India".** The Harappan "empire" stops at Meerut. Think of it as the last Harappan "railway station" heading east.
3. **Rigveda numbers: "10 floors, 1028 rooms".** The Rigveda is a 10-storey hostel with about 1,028 rooms. The Gayatri lives on the **3rd floor** ("Gaya-**tri**" = *tri* = 3). The **Purusha** (whole society, four varnas) lives on the **top floor, 10**.
4. **Darshana founders: "Kapil Pat-Gauta Kana Jaimini Badar".** Read the schools in order: **S**ankhya–**Y**oga–**N**yaya–**V**aisheshika–**M**imamsa–**V**edanta = "**Six Yogis Never Value Money, Value** wisdom." The founders follow the same order: Kapila, Patanjali, Gautama, Kanada, Jaimini, Badarayana.
5. **Kanada = atom.** "**Kan**" in Hindi means a particle (*kan*). So Vaisheshika is India's atomic theory.
6. **Temple styles as hats.** Nagara wears a **tall beehive cap** (shikhara). Dravida wears a **stepped pyramid crown** (vimana) and has a huge **gate** (gopuram). Vesara is a **mixed hat**, found in the middle of the map (Karnataka).
7. **Konark trap.** "Konark is in the **East**, so it's not **South** Dravida." It is Kalinga, a form of Nagara.
8. **Kathak = "katha" + Lucknow nawabs.** Picture Wajid Ali Shah watching a storyteller spin in *chakkars* in a Lucknow court. The four gharanas are "**L J B R**" = "**L**ucknow **J**aipur **B**anaras **R**aigarh", or "**Let's Join Birju's Rehearsal**".
9. **Sattriya = Sattra (Assam monastery).** Sankardev started it in Vaishnava *sattras*, so it is the "monastery dance".
10. **UNESCO number trick for 2026: "44 + Sarnath = 45".** The Buddha's first sermon became India's newest site, so the **first** sermon made **the latest** site. The year is 2026 and the venue is **Busan**: "**B**uddha **in B**usan".
11. **UP's UNESCO sites: "TAFS".** **T**aj, **A**gra Fort, **F**atehpur Sikri, **S**arnath. "UP's heritage is **TAFS** (tough) to beat."
12. **Kumbh 2017.** Kumbh comes every 12 years, and "**12** years needs a **17**-digit crowd count". Kumbh = 2017; Ramlila = 2008 (older, Ram is older).
13. **Amir Khusrau ≠ Akbar.** Khusrau is a **Khalji**-era poet ("**K**husrau–**K**halji"), about 250 years before Akbar. Tansen is the Akbar musician.

## 3. Don't mix up
| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| Alamgirpur vs Sinauli | Both are in western UP. Alamgirpur (Meerut) is the easternmost Harappan site. Sinauli (Baghpat) is known for its chariot burials |
| Sarnath vs Kushinagar | Sarnath is the first sermon; Kushinagar is the Mahaparinirvana (death) |
| Nyaya vs Vaisheshika | Nyaya (Gautama) is logic; Vaisheshika (Kanada) is atoms |
| Sankhya vs Yoga | Sankhya (Kapila) is theory/dualism; Yoga (Patanjali) is practice |
| Kathakali vs Kathak | Kathakali is Kerala (dance-drama, masks and make-up); Kathak is UP (footwork, spins) |
| Mohiniyattam vs Kathakali | Both are Kerala. Mohiniyattam is a graceful solo by women; Kathakali is a vigorous dance-drama by men |
| Tulsidas vs Surdas | Tulsidas: Awadhi, Rama. Surdas: Braj, Krishna |
| Taj Mahal vs Agra Fort | Both were inscribed in 1983 and are separate sites |
| World Heritage vs Intangible Heritage | Sarnath (2026) is a site; Kumbh (2017) is intangible heritage |
| Dholavira (2021) vs Rakhigarhi | Dholavira is a UNESCO site; Rakhigarhi is the largest Harappan site but is not inscribed |

## 4. Exam traps
- "Lion Capital" is from **Sarnath**, not Sanchi. Sanchi is in MP and is famous for its stupa and toranas.
- The Gayatri Mantra is addressed to **Savitri** (the Sun), not Gayatri the goddess.
- Konark and Lingaraja are **Nagara (Kalinga)**, not Dravida.
- The Sangeet Natak Akademi list has **8** classical dances (some lists add Chhau as a 9th, but the standard answer is 8).
- India's UNESCO count is **45** (as of November 2026). Answer keys printed before July 2026 will say 44 and older ones 43 or 42, so read the question's date.
- Ramcharitmanas is in **Awadhi**, not Hindi (Khari Boli) or Braj.
- Purandara Dasa is the "father of Carnatic music"; Tyagaraja belongs to the Carnatic **Trinity**.
