### Physiographic divisions (6)
1. The Himalaya (Himadri / Greater, Himachal / Lesser, Shiwalik / Outer)
2. The Northern Plains (Bhabar → Terai → Bhangar (old alluvium) → Khadar (new alluvium))
3. The Peninsular Plateau (Deccan, Malwa, Chhota Nagpur; Western and Eastern Ghats)
4. The Indian Desert (Thar)
5. The Coastal Plains (west: Konkan, Kanara, Malabar; east: Northern Circars, Coromandel)
6. The Islands (Andaman & Nicobar: volcanic/tectonic; Lakshadweep: coral)

**Panch Prayag (going downstream on the Alaknanda):** Vishnuprayag (Dhauliganga) → Nandprayag (Nandakini) → Karnaprayag (Pindar) → Rudraprayag (Mandakini) → **Devprayag (Bhagirathi) = Ganga**.

### Climate and monsoon
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Climate type | Tropical monsoon |
| Monsoon onset in Kerala | about **1 June**; covers the whole country by about 8 July (revised normal) |
| Two branches of the SW monsoon | Arabian Sea branch and Bay of Bengal branch |

## 2. Memory hooks

1. **Tropic of Cancer: the "8-state belt".** Draw a belt from west to east: **G**ujarat → **R**ajasthan → **M**P → **C**hhattisgarh → **J**harkhand → **W**est Bengal → **T**ripura → **M**izoram. "**G**ood **R**oads **M**ake **C**ars **J**ourney **W**ithout **T**raffic **M**ess." Odisha, UP and Bihar are **not** on the belt.
2. **IST meridian = "Mirzapur's watch".** Imagine the whole country setting its watch at a clock tower in **Mirzapur**. The number trick for 82.5° E: 82.5 ÷ 15 = **5.5** hours, which gives IST = GMT + **5:30**.
3. **Panch Prayag: "Vishnu Nand Karna Rudra Dev" (downstream).** The god **Vishnu** starts the procession, **Nand** greets, **Karna** fights, **Rudra** dances, and finally **Dev** blesses: the Ganga is born at **Dev**prayag. The joining rivers, in the same order: "**D**hauli, **N**anda, **P**indar, **M**anda, **B**hagi" = "**D**on't **N**ap, **P**lease **M**eet **B**hagirathi."
4. **West-flowing rivers make estuaries, east-flowing make deltas (analogy).** The west-flowers (Narmada, Tapi) run fast in **rift valleys**, like water down a **narrow drain pipe**: no time to drop silt, so they form estuaries. The east-flowers (Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi) cross a gentle slope, like a **lazy river spreading on a floor**, and form deltas. "**N**armada and **T**api: **N**o **T**ime for deltas."
5. **Tamil Nadu's "late rain" (story).** The SW monsoon is a delivery boy who comes from the **west (Arabian Sea)**. The Western Ghats is a tall gate that blocks him, so Tamil Nadu waits until he **returns** (October–December) through the **Bay of Bengal** door.
6. **Soil colours, like a painter's palette.** **Alluvial** is the plain beige canvas that covers most of the picture (about 40%). **Black** is "cotton candy on basalt": black soil equals cotton. **Red** is rusty iron. **Laterite** is "**later**, after heavy rain washes everything away" (leaching).
7. **States by area: "Raj-MP-Maha-UP" (R-M-M-U).** "**R**ajasthan **M**akes **M**ore **U**nits." UP is 4th in area but **1st in people**. Think of UP as a crowded joint family in a medium-sized house.
8. **UP's neighbours: "8 + 1 + 1".** Eight states, one UT (Delhi) and one country (Nepal). Go anticlockwise from the north (via the west): "**U**ncle **H**ari **H**ad **R**ice, **M**ango, **C**hilli, **J**aggery, **B**read" = Uttarakhand, Himachal, Haryana, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Bihar.
9. **National parks as animal "addresses".** **Gir** = the lion's house (Gujarat). **Kaziranga** = the rhino's horn (Assam). **Dudhwa** = the swamp deer's Terai pond (UP). **Silent Valley** = the quiet macaque (Kerala). "**D**udhwa **D**eer, **K**aziranga **K**ing-rhino, **G**ir **G**rowl."
10. **UP tiger reserves: "Do Pandit Aaj Ramgarh?" → D-P-A-R.** **D**udhwa, **P**ilibhit, **A**mangarh and **R**anipur, the newest one (2022, Chitrakoot, Bundelkhand). Ranipur is the "Rani" (queen) who joined last.
11. **Minerals as a map story.** **Kh**etri = **K**opper (Rajasthan's copper "kettle"). **Kolar** = "**Gol**d **Kol**ar". **Singrauli** is the "coal bridge" between MP and UP (Sonbhadra), feeding the Rihand and Anpara power plants. "Sonbhadra = **Sona** (gold) of UP's minerals."
12. **Mawsynram vs Cherrapunji: "Maw is more".** Mawsynram is slightly wetter on average. Both sit on the **windward** Khasi slopes, where funnel valleys squeeze the clouds like a sponge.

## 3. Don't mix up
| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| Bhangar vs Khadar | Old alluvium (less fertile, kankar) vs new alluvium (fertile, renewed by floods) |
| Bhabar vs Terai | Porous pebble belt where streams disappear vs marshy, forested belt where they re-emerge (Dudhwa, Pilibhit) |
| Devprayag vs Rudraprayag | Bhagirathi (Ganga begins) vs Mandakini |
| Kolar vs Hutti | Closed gold field (2001) vs working gold mine, both in Karnataka |
| Anamudi vs Doddabetta | Highest peak of peninsular India vs highest peak of the Nilgiris |
| Western disturbances vs NE monsoon | Winter rain in NW India vs October–December rain in Tamil Nadu |
| Kaziranga vs Manas | Both are in Assam. Kaziranga is famous for rhinos; Manas is a tiger and pygmy hog reserve |
| Corbett (Uttarakhand) vs Dudhwa (UP) | First NP (1936) vs UP's main NP (1977) |
| Standard meridian city | Near **Mirzapur**, not Prayagraj or Varanasi |
| Godavari vs Narmada | Longest peninsular river (east, delta) vs longest west-flowing river (rift, estuary) |

## 4. Exam traps
- The Tropic of Cancer does **not** pass through **Odisha**, **UP** or **Bihar**.
- UP touches **Himachal Pradesh** (a small border in Saharanpur), so it borders **8** states. Students often miss Himachal and answer 7.
- Black soil is formed from **basalt**, not alluvium. "Self-ploughing" (it cracks when dry) is a classic clue.
- **Gir** is the **only** natural home of the Asiatic lion, and **Kuno (MP)** hosts the reintroduced cheetahs.
- The Godavari is the **longest peninsular river**, while the **Narmada** is the longest **west-flowing** river.
- "UP's 4th tiger reserve" refers to **Ranipur (Chitrakoot)**, not Ranthambore (Rajasthan).
- UP is 4th by area but 1st by population, so do not answer "largest state" with UP.
