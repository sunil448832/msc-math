### Uttar Pradesh angle
- Chipko and Tehri were movements of **undivided UP** (before Uttarakhand was formed in 2000).
- UNCCD COP14 was held at Greater Noida (2019).
- Taj Trapezium Zone (SC, *M.C. Mehta*, 1996).
- Namami Gange: Kanpur, Prayagraj and Varanasi are key stretches.
- UP has several Ramsar sites (Nawabganj/Unnao, Saman, Sandi, Sur Sarovar, Haiderpur and others), and the count kept rising in 2026. India crossed 100 Ramsar sites in 2026.

## 2. Memory hooks
1. **Rio is a birthday party where two twins were born**, UNFCCC and CBD. Agenda 21 was the guest list for the 21st century.
2. **Waste-chemical treaties: "B-R-S = Bin, Read label, Stop POPs".** **B**asel moves the **B**in (hazardous waste), **R**otterdam = **R**ead the label first (PIC), **S**tockholm **S**tops POPs. Order 1989 → 1998 → 2001.
3. **The ozone family tree.** Vienna (1985) is the parent, Montreal (1987) the child, Kigali (2016) the grandchild who fixed HFCs. "Vienna waltzes, Montreal acts, Kigali cleans up."
4. **Kyoto vs Paris = school exam vs gym membership.** Kyoto = the teacher sets marks only for senior students (Annex I). Paris = everyone sets their own fitness goal (NDC) and reports every 5 years.
5. **COP number trick.** COP1 was in 1995, so up to 2019 the year = 1994 + COP number (COP25 → 2019). No COP was held in 2020 (Covid), so from then on the year = 1995 + COP number: COP26 → 2021, COP30 → 2025, COP31 → 2026.
6. **COP30 Belém = "Mutirão".** Think "**M**utirão = **M**any hands together". Amazon city, forest COP → TFFF (forests paid to stand).
7. **COP31 = "Turkish host, Aussie driver".** Türkiye owns the car (venue Antalya); Australia drives the negotiations.
8. **NAPCC 8 missions: "SEW HAS GK".** **S**olar, **E**nergy efficiency, **W**ater, **H**imalaya, **A**griculture, **S**ustainable habitat, **G**reen India, **K**nowledge. "Sew has GK" (a tailor with general knowledge).
9. **NDC numbers climb "45 → 47", "50 → 60".** Intensity cut 45% (2030) → 47% (2035); non-fossil 50% (2030) → about 60% (2035). Net zero is the "70" in 2070.
10. **Movement map, north to south:** Chipko (hug the tree, Himalaya) → Narmada (river, central India) → Appiko (Kannada for "hug", Western Ghats) → Silent Valley (Kerala). Chipko and Appiko both mean "hug".
11. **Ramsar = 71, India = 82.** Iran signed in '71, India joined in '82: the digits swap places (7-1 / 8-2, both rising by one).

## 3. Don't mix up
| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| Stockholm Conference (1972) vs Stockholm Convention (2001) | Human environment summit vs POPs treaty |
| Basel vs Rotterdam | Wastes moving across borders vs PIC for chemicals in trade |
| Montreal vs Kigali | CFCs (ozone) vs HFCs (climate) |
| Montreal Protocol (1987) vs Kunming–Montreal GBF (2022) | Ozone vs biodiversity |
| Cartagena vs Nagoya | Biosafety (GMOs) vs access and benefit sharing |
| COP27 vs COP28 | Loss & Damage Fund vs UAE Consensus / Global Stocktake |
| COP30 host vs COP31 host | Brazil (Belém) vs Türkiye (Antalya) |
| ISA HQ vs UNEP HQ | Gurugram vs Nairobi |
| Chipko vs Appiko | Uttarakhand (then UP), 1973 vs Karnataka, 1983 |
| NAPCC mission vs later missions | Green Hydrogen (2023) and Mission LiFE are NOT NAPCC missions |

## 4. Exam traps
- The Kyoto Protocol was **not** adopted at Rio; it came 5 years later at COP3.
- The Paris Agreement came into force in **2016**, not 2015.
- Chipko is **not** a Karnataka movement, and Appiko is not Himalayan.
- India's NDC targets **emissions intensity**, not absolute emissions.
- COP31 (Nov 2026) is **upcoming**, not concluded, as of 1 Nov 2026. Do not attribute any "outcome" to it.
- The COP30 final decision **did not** include a fossil-fuel phase-out roadmap.
