## 2. Memory hooks
1. **The EPA is an umbrella.** Water and Air Acts are separate raincoats; after Bhopal, India bought one big umbrella (1986) to cover everything, from EIA to waste rules.
2. **Act years: "72-74-80-81-86" = "Wild Water Forest Air Everything".** Animals first (1972), then water (1974), forest (1980), air (1981), and the umbrella (1986). Think "**W**ild **W**ater **F**lows **A**cross **E**arth".
3. **Article 252 vs 253.** **252** = "**2** states ask" (State-List subject: water, wildlife). **253** = "**3**rd party, i.e. the world" (treaty implementation: Air Act, EPA).
4. **42 is the green number.** The 42nd Amendment gave us 48A and 51A(g). Do not mix it up with the 44th (1978), which removed the right to property from Fundamental Rights. "42 = Green, 44 = Gone property."
5. **AQI is a school report card.** 0–50 Good (A grade), 51–100 Satisfactory, 101–200 Moderate, 201–300 Poor, 301–400 Very Poor, 401–500 Severe (detention). Every step is 100, except that the first 100 is split into two halves.
6. **NGT is the "green high court on wheels".** Delhi is the head office, with four branches: **B**hopal, **P**une, **K**olkata, **C**hennai, "**B**e **P**olite, **K**eep **C**lean".
7. **EIA "A for All-India, B for Bhai (state)".** Category A is cleared by the Centre, Category B by the State.
8. **The ESP is a balloon rubbed on your hair.** Charged plates attract dust, like a static-charged balloon picks up paper bits. The cyclone separator is a salad spinner that flings out the heavy bits.
9. **BS-VI = "jump the queue".** India skipped BS-V. 50 ppm → 10 ppm sulphur: "a five-times cleaner diet".
10. **SWM 2026 = "4 bins: Wet, Dry, Sanitary, Special" ("WDSS").** "**W**hy **D**on't **S**ort **S**eparately?"

## 3. Don't mix up
| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| Art 48A vs 51A(g) | State's duty (DPSP) vs citizen's duty |
| Art 252 vs 253 | State-request route vs treaty route |
| Water Act vs Air Act | 1974, Art 252, created the boards vs 1981, Art 253, used existing boards |
| Category A vs B (EIA) | Centre vs State (SEIAA) |
| Moderate vs Poor (AQI) | 101–200 vs 201–300 |
| ESP vs cyclone separator | Fine vs coarse particles |
| Scrubber/FGD vs ESP | Gases (SO₂) vs particles |
| Water Act decriminalisation vs EPA/Air Act decriminalisation | Water Amendment Act 2024 vs Jan Vishwas Act 2023 |
| SWM Rules 2016 vs 2026 | 3-way segregation vs 4-stream segregation |

## 4. Exam traps
- 48A and 51A(g) came from the **42nd** Amendment, not the 44th.
- The CPCB was created by the **Water Act (1974)**, not by the EPA.
- NGT appeals go **directly to the Supreme Court**, not to a High Court.
- A catalytic converter **does not** remove CO₂; it produces CO₂.
- BS-V **was never** implemented in India.
- The NGT Act was passed in **2010**. The idea was recommended by the Law Commission (186th Report), but the NGT replaced the National Environment Appellate Authority, not the CPCB.
