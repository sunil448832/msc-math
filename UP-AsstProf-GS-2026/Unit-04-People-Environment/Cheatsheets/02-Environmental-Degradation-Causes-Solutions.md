## 1. Core facts

### Pollutant → disease
| Pollutant | Disease / effect |
|---|---|
| Mercury | Minamata disease (Japan, 1956) |
| Cadmium | Itai-itai ("ouch-ouch") disease |
| Nitrate | Blue baby syndrome (methaemoglobinaemia) |
| Fluoride | Fluorosis (teeth and bones) |
| Arsenic | Black foot disease, arsenicosis (skin lesions) |
| Lead | Plumbism; brain damage in children |
| Asbestos | Asbestosis |
| Silica dust | Silicosis (stone-cutters, glass workers) |
| Coal dust | Black lung disease (pneumoconiosis) |
| Cotton dust | Byssinosis |

### Air pollution
| Pollutant | Main source |
|---|---|
| SO₂ | Coal power plants, refineries → acid rain |
| NOx | Vehicles, high-temperature combustion → acid rain, smog |
| CO | Incomplete combustion; binds to haemoglobin |
| PM2.5 / PM10 | Dust, stubble burning, vehicles |
| Ground-level O₃ | Secondary pollutant (NOx + VOC + sunlight) |
| PAN | Photochemical smog |

### Water and soil
| Concept | Key point |
|---|---|
| BOD | Oxygen that microbes need for organic matter. High BOD = dirty water |
| COD | Chemical oxygen demand (always ≥ BOD) |
| Eutrophication | Excess N and P → algal bloom → low DO → fish die |
| Soil pollution | Pesticides, fertiliser overuse, industrial waste, plastics |
| UP angle | Arsenic (Ballia and other east UP districts), fluoride (Sonbhadra), chromium (Kanpur tanneries) |

### Noise limits (Noise Rules 2000), dB(A)
| Zone | Day (6 a.m.–10 p.m.) | Night |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial | 75 | 70 |
| Commercial | 65 | 55 |
| Residential | 55 | 45 |
| Silence | 50 | 40 |

### Climate change and GHGs
| Gas | Note |
|---|---|
| CO₂ | Largest anthropogenic contributor (GWP = 1) |
| CH₄ | GWP about 28 (100 yr); paddy, livestock, landfills |
| N₂O | GWP about 265; fertilisers |
| HFCs, PFCs, SF₆ | Very high GWP; SF₆ is the highest |
| Water vapour | Most abundant natural GHG |
| Kyoto basket | CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs, SF₆ (+ NF₃ via Doha) |

### Ozone
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Location | Stratosphere (about 15–35 km), the "good ozone" |
| Unit | Dobson Unit; "hole" < 220 DU |
| Culprits | CFCs, halons, CCl₄, methyl bromide (Cl and Br atoms) |
| Hole season | Antarctic spring (Sept–Oct) |
| Day | 16 September (Montreal Protocol signed 1987) |

### Acid rain, deforestation, desertification, e-waste
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Acid rain | pH < 5.6; H₂SO₄ + HNO₃; damages marble, lakes and forests |
| Taj & acid rain | Mathura refinery → Taj Trapezium Zone (about 10,400 sq km) |
| Deforestation | Causes: shifting cultivation (jhum), agriculture, mining, dams |
| Desertification | Land degradation in drylands; UNCCD 1994; Day = 17 June |
| E-waste | E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022, in force 1 April 2023, based on EPR |

## 2. Memory hooks
1. **"Ouch-ouch Cadmium" (C for Cad).** Itai-itai hurts the bones; "Cad" makes you cry.
2. **"Minamata = Mercury".** Both start with M. The fish in the bay ate mercury, and the people ate the fish.
3. **"Blue Baby Needs Nitrate-free water".** **B**lue **B**aby, the **N** in well water from fertilisers.
4. **"Black Foot walks on Arsenic".** Picture an **A**rsenic **A**lley: walk barefoot and your feet turn black.
5. **"pH 5.6 = 5 and 6 rupees".** Rain is "naturally a little sour". Below 5.6 it turns into acid.
6. **The ozone layer is the Earth's sunscreen.** CFCs are the scratch on the bottle: one chlorine atom can destroy thousands of ozone molecules.
7. **The Antarctic hole is a "spring sale".** The ingredients are stocked in the dark winter (polar clouds), and the sale opens when the spring sunlight arrives (Sept–Oct).
8. **BOD is the restaurant bill of the bacteria.** The more food (organic waste) there is, the bigger the oxygen bill, so high BOD = dirty water.
9. **Noise limits follow the "75-65-55-50" staircase.** Industry, commerce, home, silence: drop 10, 10, then 5. At night take away 5/10/10/10 (70-55-45-40).
10. **Kyoto 6 gases: "Clever Monkeys Never Hide Peanuts Secretly".** CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFC, PFC, SF₆.
11. **Greenhouse = car parked in the sun.** Sunlight gets in through the glass, but heat cannot escape.

## 3. Don't mix up
| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| Minamata vs Itai-itai | Mercury vs Cadmium |
| Stratospheric vs tropospheric ozone | Protective vs pollutant (smog) |
| Ozone depletion vs global warming | UV shield thinning (CFCs) vs heat-trapping (GHGs). CFCs do both, but these are different problems |
| BOD vs COD | Biological vs chemical oxidation; COD ≥ BOD |
| Silence zone day vs night | 50 vs 40 dB(A) |
| Arsenic (Ballia) vs Chromium (Kanpur) | Geogenic groundwater vs tannery effluent |
| 17 June vs 5 June vs 16 Sept | Desertification day vs Environment Day vs Ozone Day |
| Chernobyl vs Fukushima | 1986, USSR (Ukraine) vs 2011, Japan |

## 4. Exam traps
- **High** BOD = **polluted** (not clean).
- Water vapour **is** a greenhouse gas.
- Normal rain is **slightly acidic** (about 5.6), not neutral (pH 7).
- Eutrophication is caused by **N and P**, not by heavy metals.
- The ozone hole is worst in the Antarctic **spring**, not in winter.
- Arsenic in the Gangetic plain is mostly **natural (geogenic)**.
