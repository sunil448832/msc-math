### Ecosystem basics
| Item | Key point |
|---|---|
| Producers → consumers → decomposers | Energy flows one way; nutrients cycle |
| Grazing food chain | Starts from green plants |
| Detritus food chain | Starts from dead organic matter (main chain in forests) |
| Food web | Interlinked food chains, which make an ecosystem stable |
| Energy pyramid | ALWAYS upright |
| Biomass pyramid | Inverted in ponds and oceans (phytoplankton < zooplankton at any one time) |
| Number pyramid | Inverted in a tree ecosystem (1 tree supports many insects) |
| Biomagnification | Rises up the chain: DDT, mercury (fat-soluble, persistent) |
| Ecotone | Transition zone between two ecosystems; edge effect = more species there |
| Keystone species | Has an effect far bigger than its numbers (e.g. tiger, fig tree) |

## 2. Memory hooks
1. **Energy is like a 10% tax.** Each time energy moves up a level, you keep only one rupee in ten. By the fifth level there is almost nothing left, which is why food chains are short.
2. **Name order "H-E-T-L" (Haeckel → Elton → Tansley → Lindeman)** gives the century of ecology in chronological order: the word (1866), the chain (1927), the system (1935), the 10% law (1942). Think "**Har Ek Tree Lives**".
3. **Hotspot = "1500 and 70"**, like a cricket score: 1,500 endemic plants, 70% lost. *Rich but in danger.*
4. **India's 4 hotspots, "HI-WS".** **H**imalaya, **I**ndo-Burma, **W**estern Ghats, **S**undaland. "India is HI-WS (highways) for biodiversity."
5. **Biomagnification is a fat sponge.** DDT dissolves in fat, not in water, so it is never flushed out and builds up in each predator. The eagle at the top eats the "sum" of all its prey.
6. **Pyramids.** Energy is a law of physics, so it is never upside-down. Biomass flips in water ("fish outweigh plankton at any moment"). Numbers flip on a tree.
7. **Disaster chain of command.** "PM at the top, CM in the state, DM in the district." P-C-D, like the alphabet climbing down.
8. **Hyogo before Sendai.** Both are Japanese cities. Hyogo (2005) came before Sendai (2015): **H** comes before **S** in the alphabet, and 10 years apart.
9. **Brundtland is the "grandparent's promise".** Use today's resources without stealing from the grandchildren's plate.
10. **Tragedy of the Commons is the village pond.** Everyone lets their buffalo graze and nobody repairs the pond, so the common resource dies.

## 3. Don't mix up
| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| Haeckel vs Tansley | Ecology (1866) vs Ecosystem (1935) |
| Richter vs Mercalli | Magnitude (energy, instrumental) vs Intensity (damage, observed) |
| Saffir–Simpson vs Fujita | Hurricane vs Tornado |
| Hyogo vs Sendai | 2005–15 vs 2015–30 |
| *Limits to Growth* vs *Our Common Future* | Club of Rome 1972 vs Brundtland 1987 |
| Western Ghats vs Eastern Ghats | Hotspot vs not a hotspot |
| Nawabganj (Unnao) vs Nawabganj (Bareilly) | The bird sanctuary is in Unnao |
| SDMA chair | Chief Minister, not the Governor |

## 4. Exam traps
- "The energy pyramid can be inverted": always false.
- DDT is **fat-soluble**, not water-soluble.
- Sundaland counts as an Indian hotspot only because of the **Nicobar Islands**.
- MDGs had 8 goals and SDGs have 17 goals (169 targets). Do not swap them.
- The detritus chain, not the grazing chain, carries most of the energy in a forest.
