## Solutions
**Q1 — (C).** Methylmercury from a chemical factory's effluent built up in the fish of Minamata Bay. *Also remember:* the Minamata Convention on Mercury was adopted in 2013 and came into force in 2017.

**Q2 — (A).** Itai-itai = cadmium; blue baby (methaemoglobinaemia) = nitrate; fluorosis = fluoride; black foot disease = arsenic. *Also remember:* lead poisoning is called plumbism, and "Itai-itai" means "ouch-ouch" in Japanese because of the bone pain.

**Q3 — (B).** Normal rain is already slightly acidic (about pH 5.6) because CO₂ dissolves to form carbonic acid. Rain is called "acid rain" below this value, mainly because of H₂SO₄ and HNO₃ formed from SO₂ and NOx. *Also remember:* acid rain was first described by Robert Angus Smith (1872).

**Q4 — (A).** R gives the exact mechanism behind A. *Also remember:* ozone is measured in Dobson Units, and an "ozone hole" means a column below 220 DU. World Ozone Day is 16 September.

**Q5 — (B).** Methane's 100-year GWP is roughly 28 times that of CO₂ (IPCC AR5). SF₆ is one of the six Kyoto gases. Water vapour is actually the most abundant natural greenhouse gas, so statement 3 is false. *Also remember:* the six Kyoto gases are CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs and SF₆; NF₃ was added by the Doha Amendment.

**Q6 — (D).** SO₂ from the Mathura refinery forms acid deposits that corrode marble (CaCO₃). This led to the Taj Trapezium Zone (TTZ), set up following the Supreme Court case *M.C. Mehta v. Union of India* (1996 order). *Also remember:* the TTZ covers about 10,400 sq km around the Taj.

**Q7 — (C).** The day-time limit for a silence zone is 50 dB(A); 40 dB(A) is its night-time limit. *Also remember:* "day" means 6 a.m. to 10 p.m.; a silence zone is the area within 100 m of hospitals, educational institutions and courts.

**Q8 — (A).** EPR makes producers responsible for collecting and channelising end-of-life products. *Also remember:* the current E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 came into force on 1 April 2023 and introduced EPR certificates traded through a CPCB portal.

**Q9 — (D).** R is the correct definition, but A is false: high BOD means more organic pollution, so the water is dirtier. *Also remember:* clean water has a BOD below about 5 mg/L; eutrophication (excess N and P) causes algal blooms that lower dissolved oxygen.

**Q10 — (C).** Minamata (1956) → Bhopal (2–3 December 1984, methyl isocyanate, Union Carbide) → Chernobyl (April 1986, then USSR, now Ukraine) → Fukushima (March 2011, Japan). *Also remember:* the Bhopal tragedy led to the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986.

**Q11 — (D).** The UN Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD) was adopted on 17 June 1994. *Also remember:* 5 June = World Environment Day, 22 April = Earth Day, 16 September = World Ozone Day. India hosted UNCCD COP14 at Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh (September 2019).

**Q12 — (B).** Arsenic in eastern UP districts such as Ballia comes mainly from natural (geogenic) sources in the alluvial aquifers, not tanneries. Fluoride is reported in Sonbhadra and other districts. *Also remember:* tanneries (Jajmau, Kanpur) are a source of **chromium** pollution in the Ganga.
