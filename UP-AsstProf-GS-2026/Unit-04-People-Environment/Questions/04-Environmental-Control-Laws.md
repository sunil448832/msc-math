## Solutions
**Q1 — (B).** The Bhopal gas leak (December 1984) exposed gaps in the law. The EPA (an "umbrella Act") was passed under Article 253 to implement the Stockholm decisions. *Also remember:* the Bhopal tragedy also led to the Public Liability Insurance Act, 1991, and the Supreme Court's "absolute liability" principle (*M.C. Mehta*, oleum gas leak, 1986).

**Q2 — (C).** The NGT was set up under the NGT Act, 2010 (established 18 October 2010). Its Principal Bench is in New Delhi, and its zonal benches are at Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata and Chennai. *Also remember:* the NGT must try to dispose of cases within 6 months, and appeals against its orders go to the Supreme Court.

**Q3 — (D).** The CPCB National AQI has six categories: Good (0–50), Satisfactory (51–100), Moderate (101–200), Poor (201–300), Very Poor (301–400) and Severe (401–500). *Also remember:* the National AQI was launched in 2014 and covers 8 pollutants (PM10, PM2.5, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃, NH₃, Pb).

**Q4 — (A).** Both Articles were inserted by the **42nd** Amendment (1976), not the 44th. *Also remember:* the 42nd Amendment also moved "forests" and "protection of wild animals and birds" from the State List to the Concurrent List.

**Q5 — (A).** Water is a State subject (Entry 17, State List), so Parliament could legislate only after state legislatures passed resolutions under Article 252. R therefore explains A. *Also remember:* the Air Act 1981 and the EPA 1986 were passed under Article 253 (implementing international decisions, i.e. Stockholm 1972).

**Q6 — (C).** Wild Life Act 1972 → Water Act 1974 → Forest (Conservation) Act 1980 → Air Act 1981. *Also remember:* the Water Act created the CPCB and the SPCBs; the Air Act later gave the same boards air-pollution powers.

**Q7 — (B).** BS-VI was applied nationwide from 1 April 2020; Delhi received BS-VI fuel earlier (April 2018). *Also remember:* BS-VI limits sulphur in fuel to 10 ppm (BS-IV allowed 50 ppm).

**Q8 — (D).** A cyclone separator removes coarse particles by centrifugal force; it does not remove gases. *Also remember:* flue-gas desulphurisation (FGD) units, which use limestone, remove SO₂ at thermal power plants, and electrostatic precipitators can remove over 99% of particulates.

**Q9 — (D).** All three are correct. The Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam rename came with the 2023 amendment. The Wildlife Amendment 2022 cut the Schedules from six to four and added a CITES Schedule. The Jan Vishwas Act 2023 decriminalised many offences under the EPA and the Air Act, and the Water Act was decriminalised separately by the Water Amendment Act, 2024 (as of 2026). *Also remember:* the Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023 also decriminalised offences under the 2002 Act.

**Q10 — (C).** Category A projects are appraised by the Expert Appraisal Committee and cleared by the Central Government (MoEFCC). Category B projects are cleared at the State level by the SEIAA. *Also remember:* the first EIA notification was issued in 1994 under the EPA; the four stages are screening, scoping, public consultation and appraisal.

**Q11 — (B).** The SWM Rules 2026 (notified January 2026, replacing the 2016 Rules) require four streams: wet, dry, sanitary and special-care waste (as of 2026). *Also remember:* India banned identified single-use plastic items from 1 July 2022 under the Plastic Waste Management Rules.

**Q12 — (A).** Sections 25–26 of the Water Act require the SPCB's consent to establish and to operate before effluent is discharged into a stream. *Also remember:* the Air Act has a similar consent requirement (Section 21); the UP Pollution Control Board was first set up in 1975 under the Water Act.
