## Solutions
**Q1 — (A).** Art 324 covers elections to Parliament, State Legislatures and the offices of President and Vice-President. *Also remember:* elections to panchayats and municipalities are run by **State Election Commissions** (Art 243K and 243ZA), not by the ECI.

**Q2 — (C).** Sukumar Sen (1950–58) conducted the first two general elections (1951–52 and 1957). *Also remember:* T.N. Seshan (10th CEC, 1990–96) is known for enforcing the Model Code of Conduct and introducing voter identity cards (EPIC).

**Q3 — (D).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. The 2023 Act replaced the CJI (who had been placed on the panel by the Supreme Court's *Anoop Baranwal* judgment, March 2023, as an interim arrangement) with a Union Cabinet Minister. *Also remember:* under the Act, the CEC and ECs hold office for 6 years or until age 65, whichever is earlier, and cannot be reappointed.

**Q4 — (A).** Art 324(5) itself gives the CEC the same removal protection as an SC judge (address by both Houses with special majority). *Also remember:* the 2023 Act repeated this protection, and it also protects CEC/ECs from civil and criminal proceedings for acts done in official duty.

**Q5 — (B).** Art 325 single general electoral roll, no exclusion; Art 326 adult suffrage; Art 327 Parliament's power on elections; Art 328 State Legislature's power; Art 329 bar on courts (election disputes go only by election petition). *Also remember:* the Representation of the People Act, 1950 deals with electoral rolls and seats; the 1951 Act deals with the conduct of elections and disqualifications.

**Q6 — (D).** ECI constituted 25 January 1950 → 61st Amendment Act, 1988 (in force March 1989) → first multi-member ECI, October 1989 → NOTA after the *PUCL* judgment, September 2013. *Also remember:* the ECI has been a three-member body since 1993; 25 January is celebrated as **National Voters' Day** (since 2011).

**Q7 — (B).** SPSC members: 6 years or 62 years. UPSC members: 6 years or 65 years. *Also remember:* SPSC members are appointed by the **Governor** but can be removed only by the **President** (Art 317).

**Q8 — (D).** Expenses of PSCs are charged on the Consolidated Fund under **Art 322**; Art 323 deals with **reports** (UPSC to the President, SPSC to the Governor). *Also remember:* Art 319 bars the UPSC Chairman from any further government employment; an SPSC Chairman may become UPSC Chairman or member.

**Q9 — (B).** Gyanesh Kumar took charge as the 26th CEC on 19 February 2025 and was in office as of October 2026; he was the first CEC appointed under the 2023 Act. *Also remember:* the two Election Commissioners as of October 2026 are Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi; Rajiv Kumar was the 25th CEC.

**Q10 — (A).** The UP Education Service Selection Commission Act, 2023 created UPESSC (headquartered at Prayagraj) for recruitment of teachers in aided higher-education and secondary institutions (and basic education), taking over from the UP Higher Education Service Commission and the UP Secondary Education Service Selection Board. *Also remember:* UPSSSC (Lucknow) recruits Group 'C' posts and is unrelated to teacher recruitment.

**Q11 — (C).** UPPSC was established on 1 April 1937 at Allahabad (Prayagraj), under the Government of India Act, 1935. *Also remember:* the first Public Service Commission of India was set up on 1 October 1926 (Chairman Sir Ross Barker) on the recommendation of the Lee Commission.

**Q12 — (C).** The Act provides that if there is no recognised Leader of Opposition, the leader of the single largest opposition party in the Lok Sabha is treated as the LoP for this purpose. *Also remember:* the selection is not invalid merely because of a vacancy in the Selection Committee, and the committee may consider names outside the Search Committee's panel.
