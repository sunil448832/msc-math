## Solutions
**Q1 — (C).** Under Articles 64 and 89, the Vice-President is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha. *Also remember:* the Vice-President is elected by an electoral college of **all** members of both Houses (elected and nominated), unlike the President.

**Q2 — (B).** Statements 1–3 are correct. Statement 4 is wrong: there is **no joint sitting** for a Money Bill (or a Constitution Amendment Bill). *Also remember:* a Money Bill needs the President's prior recommendation; the President cannot return it for reconsideration.

**Q3 — (A).** Art 72 pardon; Art 123 ordinance; Art 143 advisory opinion of the SC; Art 356 President's Rule. *Also remember:* the Governor's equivalents are Art 161 (pardon) and Art 213 (ordinance); the Governor cannot pardon a death sentence.

**Q4 — (C).** A is true: Art 360 has never been used. R is false: Art 360 was part of the original Constitution. *Also remember:* National Emergency (Art 352) has been proclaimed three times — 1962, 1971 and 1975.

**Q5 — (D).** The electoral college (Art 54) has elected members of both Houses of Parliament and elected members of State Legislative Assemblies, including Delhi and Puducherry (added by the 70th Amendment, 1992). Members of Legislative Councils and nominated members are excluded. *Also remember:* disputes about the President's election are decided by the Supreme Court only (Art 71).

**Q6 — (D).** Chinese aggression (Oct 1962) → Indo-Pak war (Dec 1971) → internal disturbance (June 1975) → 44th Amendment (1978). *Also remember:* in June 1975 the 1971 emergency was still in force, so two emergencies ran at the same time.

**Q7 — (A).** SC judges retire at 65 (Art 124); High Court judges at 62 (Art 217). *Also remember:* the sanctioned strength of the SC is 34 (CJI + 33) since the 2019 amendment of the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act.

**Q8 — (B).** It was set up in 1866 at **Agra** as the High Court for the North-Western Provinces and shifted to Allahabad in 1869. *Also remember:* its Lucknow Bench was set up in 1948 after the Oudh Chief Court was merged into it.

**Q9 — (D).** All three are correct. *Also remember:* UP has the largest share in Parliament — 80 Lok Sabha seats and 31 Rajya Sabha seats; it is one of six States with a Legislative Council (with Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana).

**Q10 — (B).** The Speaker presides over a joint sitting (Art 108 and Art 118(4)); in the Speaker's absence the Deputy Speaker, and then the Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha. *Also remember:* only three Bills have been passed at joint sittings: Dowry Prohibition (1961), Banking Service Commission (Repeal) (1978) and POTA (2002).

**Q11 — (C).** The 91st Amendment (2003) added Art 75(1A) and 164(1A) (15% cap; States minimum 12 ministers) and tightened anti-defection rules. *Also remember:* the 52nd Amendment (1985) introduced the Tenth Schedule (anti-defection law).

**Q12 — (A).** Because Art 74(1) makes ministerial advice binding, the President is a nominal head and real power lies with the Council of Ministers. *Also remember:* the 44th Amendment allows the President to send advice back **once** for reconsideration; the President must then act on the advice given after reconsideration.
