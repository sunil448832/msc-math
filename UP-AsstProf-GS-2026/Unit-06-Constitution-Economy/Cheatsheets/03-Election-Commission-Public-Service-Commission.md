## 1. Core facts

### Elections — Part XV (Art 324–329)
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| 324 | Superintendence, direction, control of elections (Parliament, State Legislatures, President, Vice-President) vested in ECI; 324(5) removal protection of CEC |
| 325 | One general electoral roll per constituency; no exclusion on religion, race, caste, sex |
| 326 | Adult suffrage (18 years since 61st Amendment, 1988) |
| 327 | Parliament's power to legislate on elections |
| 328 | State Legislature's power to legislate on its elections |
| 329 | Bar on courts' interference; disputes only through election petition |

### Election Commission of India
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Constituted | **25 Jan 1950** (National Voters' Day since 2011) |
| Composition | CEC + 2 ECs (multi-member first in Oct 1989; permanently since 1993) |
| Decisions | By majority |
| Appointment law | **CEC and Other ECs (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act, 2023** |
| Selection Committee | **PM + LoP in Lok Sabha (or leader of single largest opposition party) + Union Cabinet Minister nominated by PM** |
| Search Committee | Headed by Union **Law Minister** + 2 Secretary-level officers; panel of 5 names |

| Tenure | 6 years or 65 years of age, whichever earlier |
| Removal | CEC: like an SC judge. ECs: only on CEC's recommendation |
| First CEC | **Sukumar Sen** (1950–58) |
| Famous reformer | T.N. Seshan (10th CEC): MCC enforcement, EPIC voter ID |
| CEC (as of Oct 2026) | **Gyanesh Kumar**, 26th CEC, since 19 Feb 2025 (first under the 2023 Act) |
| ECs (as of Oct 2026) | **Sukhbir Singh Sandhu** (since Mar 2024), **Vivek Joshi** (since Feb 2025) |

## 2. Memory hooks
1. **Referee analogy.** The ECI is the match referee of democracy: it does not play for any team, it supervises the whole game (Art 324), and only the "Parliament-level" disciplinary process (same as an SC judge) can send it off the field.
2. **Articles 324–329 "Control, Roll, Age, Parliament, State, Stop".** 324 Control → 325 Roll → 326 Age (adult suffrage) → 327 Parliament → 328 State → 329 Stop (courts). Mnemonic: "**C**lever **R**oll **A**ll **P**eople **S**afely **S**top".
3. **Number trick 324 → 3 members.** Article **3**24 and a **3**-member Commission: easy pair.
4. **2023 Selection Committee = "PM + 2"**, a trio where the PM has a majority (PM + his minister). The CJI was "**replaced by the PM's teammate**", which is the exam point.
5. **PSC Articles 315–323 = "Make, Appoint, Remove, Rules, Retire, Work, Extra, Pay, Report".** Story: a commission is made (315), people are appointed (316), one is removed (317), rules are written (318), retirees are barred from new jobs (319), work is done (320), extra work added (321), salary paid from the Consolidated Fund (322) and a report card goes up (323).
6. **"Governor hires, President fires."** SPSC members are appointed by the Governor but only the President can remove them.
7. **Ages "65 for Union, 62 for State"**, the same as SC (65) and HC (62) judges. Union = Supreme = 65; State = High Court = 62.
8. **"1926–1937–1950" staircase:** PSC → Federal PSC and UPPSC (both 1937) → UPSC. "UP got its own PSC the same year the Centre got a Federal PSC."
9. **First CEC "Sukumar Sen — Sukh se pehla chunav".** He made the first election go peacefully ("sukh se").
10. **UPESSC = "Ek chhat, teen manzil".** One roof (Prayagraj) for three floors of teachers: basic, secondary and higher education.

## 3. Don't mix up
| Item | vs | Item |
|---|---|---|
| ECI (Art 324): Parliament, Assemblies, President, VP | | State Election Commission (Art 243K): panchayat and municipal polls |
| *Anoop Baranwal* (2023 SC): PM + LoP + CJI | | 2023 Act: PM + LoP + Cabinet Minister |
| Art 322: expenses of PSCs | | Art 323: reports of PSCs |
| UPSC member age 65 | | SPSC member age 62 |
| RPA 1950: electoral rolls, seats | | RPA 1951: conduct of elections, disqualifications, election petitions |
| UPPSC (1937): State civil services | | UPESSC (2023 Act): teachers and Assistant Professors |
| Search Committee: headed by Law Minister | | Selection Committee: headed by PM |

## 4. Exam traps
- The CJI is **not** on the CEC selection committee under the 2023 Act.
- Other ECs can be removed by the President on the CEC's **recommendation**; they do not have the same protection as the CEC.
- Members of an SPSC are removed by the **President**, never by the Governor.
- The Model Code of Conduct is **not** a statute; it is a consensus code enforced by the ECI.
- The ECI also conducts the **Presidential and Vice-Presidential** elections, but **not** local body elections.
- The first EVM use was in **Kerala (1982)**, not in a Lok Sabha election.
- "Voting age 18" came through the **61st** Amendment, not the 62nd or 42nd.
