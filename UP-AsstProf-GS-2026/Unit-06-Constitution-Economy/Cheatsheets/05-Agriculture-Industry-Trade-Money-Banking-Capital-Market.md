### Money and currency
| Measure | Composition | Liquidity |
|---|---|---|
| M0 (reserve money) | Currency in circulation + bankers' deposits with RBI + other deposits with RBI | |
| **M1** (narrow) | Currency with public + demand deposits + other deposits with RBI | **Most liquid** |
| M2 | M1 + savings deposits of post office savings banks | |
| **M3** (broad) | M1 + time deposits with banks | |
| M4 | M3 + total post office deposits (excl. NSC) | **Least liquid** |

- RBI issues all notes except **₹1** (Govt; signed by **Finance Secretary**). Coins are minted by the Govt (4 mints: Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Noida).
- Minimum Reserve System since 1957 (₹200 crore, of which gold ₹115 crore).
- ₹ symbol: D. Udaya Kumar, adopted 15 July 2010. Demonetisation of ₹500/₹1000: 8 Nov 2016.

| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Established | **1 April 1935** under RBI Act 1934 (Hilton Young Commission); HQ Kolkata → Mumbai 1937; nationalised **1 Jan 1949** |
| Governor (as of Oct 2026) | **Sanjay Malhotra** (26th, since Dec 2024) |

| Inflation target | CPI **4% ± 2%**, retained for **Apr 2026 – Mar 2031** |

**Policy rates (as of October 2026, after the MPC meeting of 7 Oct 2026):**
| Rate | Level |
|---|---|
| Repo | **5.50%** (raised 25 bps from 5.25%) |
| SDF (floor of corridor) | 5.25% |
| MSF (ceiling) | 5.75% |
| Bank Rate | 5.75% |
| CRR | 3% |
| SLR | 18% |
| Stance | "Calibrated tightening" (changed from neutral) |

| Tool | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Repo | RBI lends to banks against govt securities (short term) |
| Reverse repo / SDF | Banks park surplus with RBI; SDF needs no collateral (since April 2022) |
| MSF | Overnight emergency borrowing by banks, can use SLR securities |
| Bank Rate | Long-term lending rate; aligned with MSF |
| CRR | % of NDTL kept as **cash with RBI** (Sec 42, RBI Act) |
| SLR | % of NDTL kept **by the bank itself** as liquid assets (Sec 24, BR Act) |
| OMO | Buying (inject) / selling (absorb) govt securities |
| Qualitative tools | Margin requirements, credit rationing, moral suasion, selective credit control |

## 2. Memory hooks
1. **The RBI as a water-tank.** The RBI is the tank, banks are the taps. **Repo** is the RBI letting water into the taps (at a price); **CRR** is water that must stay locked in the tank; **SLR** is water the tap must keep in its own bucket; **OMO selling** drains water from the pipes. Inflation = too much water → close the valves (raise repo, sell bonds).
2. **Corridor "5.25 – 5.50 – 5.75".** SDF floor, repo in the middle, MSF on top: a staircase with 25-paise steps (as of October 2026).
3. **"CRR Cash, SLR Self".** CRR is Cash with RBI; SLR is kept by the bank itSelf.
4. **Money measures as a ladder of liquidity.** M1 is cash in your pocket (most liquid); M4 is money locked in the post office (least liquid). "As the number goes up, the money slows down."
5. **"1-2-3-4 Banking story: 35-55-69-82".** RBI born 1935, SBI 1955, nationalisation 1969, NABARD 1982. "The RBI was a baby in 35, SBI a teenager in 55, nationalised at 69, and grew a farmer's arm (NABARD) at 82."
6. **"Prathama = Pehla".** Prathama means "first": the first RRB, at Moradabad, UP, on Gandhi Jayanti 1975.
7. **Sensex vs Nifty "30 vs 50".** "**S**ensex = **S**mall (30) on the BSE; **N**ifty = **N**ifty fifty on the **N**SE."
8. **Industrial policy "48 Mix, 56 Schedule, 91 Free".** 1948 mixed the economy, 1956 scheduled it (A-B-C), 1991 freed it (LPG).
9. **₹1 note = "Govt's own child".** The ₹1 note is the only note the Government issues itself, so its own officer (Finance Secretary) signs it.
10. **Trade deals by date "EFTA Oct 25, UK Jul 26, EU pending".** "Switzerland first (2025), Britain next (2026), Europe still in the queue."
11. **UP's money map "SIDBI–Lucknow, Mint–Noida, Prathama–Moradabad".** "UP lends to small firms (SIDBI, Lucknow), makes coins (Noida mint) and opened India's first rural bank (Moradabad)." NABARD and EXIM stay in Mumbai.

## 4. Exam traps
- In October 2026 the RBI **raised** the repo rate (5.25% → 5.50%); don't assume the 2025 easing cycle is still on.
- MSF is **above** repo and SDF is **below** repo.
- The ₹1 note is **not** signed by the RBI Governor.
- M4 is the **least** liquid, not the broadest "most liquid" measure.
- First bank nationalisation was **14** banks in **1969**; the 1980 round was **6** banks.
- SIDBI is in **Lucknow**; NABARD and EXIM Bank are in Mumbai.
- The MPC has **6** members, and the Governor has a **casting** vote only in a tie.
