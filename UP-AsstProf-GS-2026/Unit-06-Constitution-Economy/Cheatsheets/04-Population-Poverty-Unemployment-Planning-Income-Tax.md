### Poverty
| Committee | Year | Key point |
|---|---|---|

| Lakdawala | 1993 | State-specific lines; CPI-AL (rural), CPI-IW (urban) |

**Multidimensional Poverty (MPI):** global MPI by UNDP + OPHI (since 2010), 3 dimensions, 10 indicators. **NITI Aayog National MPI: 3 dimensions, 12 indicators** (adds maternal health and bank account). NITI discussion paper (Jan 2024): MPI headcount **29.17% (2013-14) → 11.28% (2022-23)**; **24.82 crore** escaped; **UP largest (5.94 crore)**, then Bihar (3.77 crore), MP (2.30 crore).

### Unemployment
| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Disguised | More workers than needed; MP ≈ 0 | 6 family members on a farm needing 3 |
| Seasonal | Idle in off-season | Farm labour between harvests |
| Frictional | Between jobs | Person switching jobs |
| Structural | Mismatch of skills/technology and jobs | Typists after computers |
| Cyclical | Due to recession | Layoffs in slowdown |
| Educated | Educated but jobless/underemployed | Graduates without suitable jobs |

**PLFS** (NSO/MoSPI, launched 2017). Activity statuses: **Usual Status** (365 days), **Current Weekly Status** (7 days), **Current Daily Status**. Monthly bulletin since 2025 (CWS). As of October 2026: September 2026 unemployment rate (15+, CWS) = **5.0%**.

### Income Tax
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Nature | Direct, progressive tax |
| Levy | Union (Union List entry 82); agricultural income → States (State List entry 46) |
| Sharing | Through Finance Commission (Art 270) |
| Administration | CBDT (Central Boards of Revenue Act, 1963), Department of Revenue |
| Constitutional rule | Art 265: no tax without authority of law |
| **Income-tax Act, 2025** | Assent **21 Aug 2025**; in force **1 Apr 2026**; 536 sections, 23 chapters, 16 schedules; replaced the 1961 Act (819 sections, 47 chapters); **"tax year"** replaces previous year/assessment year |
| New regime | Default since FY 2023-24 |

**New regime slabs (as of October 2026; unchanged in Budget 2026):**
| Income (₹) | Rate |
|---|---|
| 0 – 4 lakh | Nil |
| 4 – 8 lakh | 5% |
| 8 – 12 lakh | 10% |
| 12 – 16 lakh | 15% |
| 16 – 20 lakh | 20% |
| 20 – 24 lakh | 25% |
| Above 24 lakh | 30% |

Rebate: tax **nil** up to ₹12 lakh taxable income (max ₹60,000). Standard deduction (salaried) **₹75,000** (new) vs ₹50,000 (old). So salary up to **₹12.75 lakh** = nil tax. Cess 4% (Health & Education).

## 2. Memory hooks
1. **Census 2011 "943–919–382–74".** Read it like a phone number: "**943** girls for 1000 boys, **919** among small kids, **382** people per sq km, **74**% can read." 
2. **UP's mirror trick.** UP density **829** vs India **382**: UP is more than double. UP sex ratio **912** is "9-1-2, below 9-4-3".
3. **Census 2027 = "Count in Spring, Snow first".** The plains are counted for 1 March 2027 (spring); the hills are counted earlier, before snow blocks the roads (1 October 2026).
4. **Poverty lines as a thermometer.** Tendulkar (21.9%) shows a lower "fever" than Rangarajan (29.5%) for the same patient (2011-12). "T for Twenty-one, R for Raised to 29.5."
5. **Unemployment as a wedding kitchen.** Disguised = 10 cooks where 5 are enough (extra cooks add nothing); seasonal = cooks idle after wedding season; frictional = a cook moving to another hotel; structural = a tandoor cook when everyone wants pizza.
6. **Plans "1-Agri, 2-Industry, 3-Fail, Holiday".** "First feed, then build, then fall, then rest." Mahalanobis = **M** for Machines (2nd Plan heavy industry).
7. **"1950 Commission, 2015 Aayog: 15 March vs 1 January."** Both start with "1": the Commission on the Ides of March, NITI on New Year's Day.
8. **New-regime slabs "4-8-12-16-20-24".** The table of 4: each slab is ₹4 lakh wide and the rate goes up by 5% per step (0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30).
9. **"12 + 0.75 = 12.75" tax-free salary.** Rebate covers 12 lakh; the standard deduction adds 75 thousand.
10. **Tax law numbers "1961 → 2025, 819 → 536".** "The old law was a fat book of 819 pages; the new one is on a diet at 536."
11. **MPI "24.82 crore, UP first".** "UP is the biggest State, so it has the biggest escape: 5.94 crore — almost 6 crore."

## 4. Exam traps
- UP's sex ratio is **below** the national average; Kerala is highest.
- Bihar, not West Bengal, has the highest population density (2011).
- NITI Aayog and the Planning Commission were both created by **executive resolution**, not by law or the Constitution.
- The 12th Plan was the **last** Five-Year Plan; NITI has a 15-year vision, 7-year strategy and 3-year action agenda instead.
- Agricultural income is **not** taxed by the Centre.
- The Income-tax Act **2025** (not 2024 or 2026) is the new law; it took effect from **1 April 2026**.
- Census 2027 Phase I (houselisting) was in **2026**; the headcount itself is in **February 2027**.
