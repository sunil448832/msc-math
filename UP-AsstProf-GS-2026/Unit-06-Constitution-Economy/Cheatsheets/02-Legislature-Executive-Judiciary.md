## 1. Core facts

### Parliament (Part V, Art 79–122)
| Item | Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha |
|---|---|---|
| Article | 81 | 80 |
| Maximum strength | 550 (530 States + 20 UTs) | 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated) |
| Present strength | 543 | 245 (233 + 12) |
| Minimum age | 25 | 30 |
| Term | 5 years (can be dissolved) | Permanent; 1/3 retire every 2 years; member term 6 years |
| Presiding officer | Speaker (Art 93) | Chairman = Vice-President (Art 64, 89) |
| UP's share | 80 seats (largest) | 31 seats (largest) |

- Parliament = **President + Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha** (Art 79).
- Gap between two sessions: max **6 months** (Art 85).
- **Money Bill (Art 110):** only in LS, President's prior recommendation, Speaker's certificate final, RS can hold **14 days**, no joint sitting.
- **Joint sitting (Art 108):** for ordinary bills only; presided by Speaker; held 3 times (1961, 1978, 2002).
- Budget = Annual Financial Statement (**Art 112**); Consolidated Fund (**Art 266**); Contingency Fund (**Art 267**); CAG (**Art 148**).
- Nominated members of RS (12): from literature, science, art, social service (Art 80(3)).

## 2. Memory hooks
1. **Parliament as a family.** The President is the head of the house who signs everything, the Lok Sabha is the earning member who controls money (Money Bills), and the Rajya Sabha is the elder who can give advice and delay but cannot stop the money (14 days).
2. **"25-30-35" age ladder.** LS 25 → RS 30 → President, VP, Governor 35. "The higher the chair, the older the sitter."
3. **Retirement "62-65".** "High Court is lower, so it retires earlier: 62; Supreme Court 65." Think "HC = 6+2, SC = 6+5."
4. **Emergency trio "352-356-360" = "National-State-Money".** 352 for the nation, 356 for a State, 360 for the purse. Number trick: 3 national emergencies, and 360 has had **zero** use, like a full circle of 360° that came back to nothing.
5. **Three national emergencies "China-Pakistan-Indira" = 62-71-75.** Think "62 for war with the dragon, 71 for war with the neighbour, 75 for war at home".
6. **Pardon: President vs Governor.** The President is the "national doctor" who can treat any case, including death and army cases; the Governor is the "district doctor" who can only reduce or postpone a death sentence, not cancel it.
7. **Joint sitting = "Speaker's stage".** The bigger House hosts the party, so its Speaker presides. Money Bills are never invited (no joint sitting).
8. **UP's numbers "80-31-403-100".** "80 MPs fly to Delhi, 31 elders sit in the Upper House, 403 MLAs fill the Lucknow Assembly, and a round 100 sit in the Council."
9. **Allahabad HC's journey "Agra → Allahabad, 1866 → 1869".** "The court took a 3-year train ride from the Taj to the Sangam."
10. **SC Articles "131-136-137-143".** "One-Three-One: One country, two states fighting" (original); "136: Special Leave" (36 looks like a leave letter); "143: President asks for advice" ("I need your 1 opinion on 43 issues").

|---|---|---|

| Impeachment of President: "violation of Constitution" | | Removal of judges: "proved misbehaviour or incapacity" |
| Money Bill (Art 110): RS 14 days | | Financial Bill (Art 117): treated like ordinary bill in RS |
| Attorney General (Art 76) | | Advocate General (Art 165) |
| Art 352 approval: 1 month | | Art 356 / 360 approval: 2 months |
| Lucknow Bench: 1948 | | Allahabad HC founding: 1866 (Agra) |

## 4. Exam traps
- The Speaker, not the Chairman of RS, presides over a joint sitting.
- Nominated MPs **can** vote for the Vice-President but **not** for the President.
- MLCs (members of Legislative Councils) have **no** role in the Presidential election.
- Financial Emergency has **never** been declared; Art 360 is original, not added by the 44th Amendment.
- The Governor holds office "during the pleasure of the President" — there is no fixed security of a 5-year term.
- Ordinances can be issued only when the Legislature (either House) is **not in session**.
- Rajya Sabha is **not** dissolved; it is a continuing chamber.
