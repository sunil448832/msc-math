## 3. Memory hooks
1. **Analogy, the ration card upgrade:** MGNREGA was a 100-day work coupon. VB-G RAM G is the "upgraded coupon" worth **125 days**, but the state now pays a bigger share of the bill (60:40 instead of the Centre paying almost everything).
2. **Census, two-step cooking:** First you **count the plates and rooms** (houselisting, April–September 2026), then you **count the guests** (population and caste, February 2027). And for the first time, guests can **fill their own name card** (self-enumeration).
3. **Ganga Expressway = "Meerut to Magh Mela":** it starts in Meerut (west UP) and ends in Prayagraj (Sangam, where the Magh Mela is held). **594 km, 12 districts**, so think "**5-9-4, twelve doors**".
4. **UP expressway family (one city pair each):** **Pu**rvanchal = **Lu**cknow–Ghazipur · **Bu**ndelkhand = Chitrakoot–Etawah · **Ga**nga = Meerut–Prayagraj. Mnemonic: "**Pu-Lu, Bu-Chi, Ga-Me**".
5. **Rashtra Prerna Sthal "trio + 65":** three Jan Sangh/BJP icons (Mookerjee founded the Jan Sangh, Upadhyaya gave "Integral Humanism", Vajpayee was the first BJP PM), 65-ft statues on 65 acres, opened on Atal's birthday (25 Dec, also celebrated as Good Governance Day).
6. **May 2026, the "S-V-S" sweep:** **S**uvendu (Bengal, first BJP CM), **V**ijay (TN, first non-Dravidian party CM in about 60 years, and the third film star CM after MGR and Jayalalithaa), **S**atheesan (Keralam, UDF back after 10 years).
7. **Keralam, the Article 3 nameplate:** changing a state's name is like changing your **house nameplate**. You ask the family (the State Legislature, whose views are not binding), but the society committee (Parliament) decides by a **simple majority**, and it is **not** an Article 368 amendment (Article 4).
8. **Jewar, "ribbon in March, wheels in June":** PM inauguration on 28 Mar 2026, first commercial flight on 15 Jun 2026 (IndiGo from Lucknow).
9. **RBI October 2026, the "car that reversed":** after a cutting cycle (to 5.25%), the RBI shifted into **reverse with a +25 bps hike to 5.50%**, the first since Feb 2023. The new stance word is "calibrated tightening".
10. **Labour Codes, "4 cupboards for 29 files":** **W**ages (2019), **I**ndustrial Relations, **S**ocial Security, **O**SH (2020): "**WISO**", the wise codes.

## 5. Exam traps
- "Dharmendra/Padma" and other award items belong to Topic 02. This sheet covers **events**.
- Nitish Kumar's 10th oath was in **Nov 2025** (the Bihar polls), not in the May 2026 round of elections.
- Kerala became **Keralam**, so in post-Aug 2026 questions the State may appear as "Keralam".
- VB-G RAM G's guarantee is **125** days, not 150. (150 days was an earlier extra allowance in drought areas and for forest-rights holders under MGNREGA.)
- Rashtra Prerna Sthal has **no** Sardar Patel statue (Patel's is the Statue of Unity in Gujarat).
- RBI October 2026 was a **hike**, so an option that says "cut" or "hold" is a trap built on the earlier 2025 policies.
