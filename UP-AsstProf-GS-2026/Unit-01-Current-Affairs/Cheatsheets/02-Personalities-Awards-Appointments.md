## 3. Memory hooks
1. **The Nobel "week calendar":** Mon Medicine → Tue Physics → Wed Chemistry → Thu Literature → Fri Peace → next Mon Economics. Mnemonic: "**My Physics Coach Likes Peaceful Evenings**" (5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12 October 2026).
2. **Optogenetics analogy:** neurons are light bulbs wired to a **remote control made of algae protein** (channelrhodopsin). Shine light and the neuron fires. That remote control won the 2026 Medicine prize.
3. **IceCube analogy:** a **one-cubic-km ice cube at the South Pole** with about 5,000 light sensors. It is like a giant freezer that "sees" ghost particles (neutrinos) from deep space. Halzen got the Nobel for building it.
4. **Chirality analogy:** molecules have left and right "hands" like gloves. Kagan and Soai showed how a tiny excess of one hand can **snowball** into almost all one hand (autocatalysis).
5. **Padma number trick "5-13-113":** 5 Vibhushan, 13 Bhushan, 113 Shri, for **131** in total (reverse the 13 and add a 1).
6. **Jnanpith Tamil trio, "A-J-V every ~25 years":** **A**kilan 1975 → **J**ayakanthan 2002 → **V**airamuthu (60th, for 2025).
7. **Phalke "South wave":** Mohanlal (Malayalam, for 2023) → Anant Nag (Kannada, for 2024). Think "**M then N**": Mohan, then Nag, alphabetical.
8. **Appointments in calendar order, "S-G-N-S":** **S**urya Kant (Nov 2025) → **G**oyal (Dec 2025) → **N**abin (Jan 2026) → **S**eth (Jun 2026): "**S**ome **G**ood **N**ews **S**oon".
9. **UP angles in awards:** N. Rajam (Padma Vibhushan, BHU Varanasi violinist), Mamta Kalia (*Jeete Jee Allahabad*), Anandiben Patel (longest-serving UP Governor). "**Violin, memoir, Raj Bhavan**".
10. **Navi Pillay, "India-to-Africa-to-Oslo":** Tamil roots → Durban judge → UN rights chief → Nobel Peace (Oslo).

## 5. Exam traps
- **"Bharat Ratna 2026"**: no new award was found for 2025 or 2026, so an option naming one is likely a distractor. The last batch was 2024 (five awardees).
- **The award year differs from the announcement year**: Jnanpith "60th, for 2025" was announced in March 2026, and Phalke "for 2024" was announced in September 2026.
- **Booker 2026** is announced on 9 November 2026, after this sheet's cut-off. Do not assume a winner.
- **Halzen** is the **sole** 2026 Physics laureate. Options listing a trio in Physics are wrong.
- **Navi Pillay** is South African (of Indian origin), not an Indian citizen.
- **Nitin Nabin** is from **Bihar** (five-time MLA), not UP.
- "Longest-serving UP Governor" = Anandiben Patel (a milestone reached in July 2025), **not** Ram Naik.
