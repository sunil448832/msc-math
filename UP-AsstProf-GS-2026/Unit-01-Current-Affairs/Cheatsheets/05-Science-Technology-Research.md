## 4. Core static science that current-affairs questions use
| Concept | One-liner |
|---|---|
| Neutrino | Nearly massless, neutral, weakly interacting ("ghost particle"); India's planned INO is at Theni, Tamil Nadu |
| Ramjet vs Scramjet | Both air-breathing. Ramjet: subsonic combustion. Scramjet: **s**upersonic **c**ombustion |
| Josephson junction | Two superconductors with a thin insulator between them; electrons tunnel through |
| Chirality | "Handedness" of molecules: L-amino acids, D-sugars in life |
| MOFs | Metal ions joined by organic linkers into porous crystals; used for CO₂ capture and harvesting water from air |
| SAR | Synthetic Aperture Radar: sees through clouds and works at night |
| GTO vs LEO | GTO is the transfer orbit to 36,000 km geostationary; LEO is roughly 160–2,000 km (the ISS is at about 400 km) |

## 5. Memory hooks
1. **Analogy, optogenetics:** It's like fitting each neuron with a smart bulb's switch. Shine light, and the neuron turns on.
2. **Analogy, peripheral tolerance (2025):** Regulatory T cells are the **referee** who stops your own players (immune cells) from fouling teammates (your own tissues).
3. **Analogy, MOFs:** a molecular **apartment block** with metal "pillars" and organic "beams", and so much empty room inside that just a few grams can have an internal surface area about the size of a football field.
4. **Analogy, IceCube:** a fishing net a kilometre wide made of ice, because neutrinos are "ghost fish" that almost never get caught.
5. **Mnemonic, Nobel 2026 order of announcement: "M-P-C"** (**M**onday Medicine, **P**hysics, **C**hemistry). "**M**y **P**hysics **C**lass."
6. **Story, Kagan–Soai:** the "one-handed universe". Soai's reaction copies itself (autocatalysis), so a tiny excess of one hand snowballs into almost 100%, much like a rumour spreading.
7. **Number trick, launch weights:** "**4.4 → 6.1**": CMS-03 at about 4,400 kg was then outdone by BlueBird at about 6,100 kg seven weeks later.
8. **UP angle:** Shubhanshu Shukla went from **Lucknow** (City Montessori School) to the ISS. "From Nawabs' city to the space station."
9. **Number trick, Artemis II:** "**1 to 10 April, 4 crew, 0 landings**."
10. **Scramjet hook:** "**S**cram = **S**upersonic **c**ombustion **r**amjet." It breathes air, like a jet that gulps air at five times the speed of sound.

## 7. Exam traps
- **Halzen** won the 2026 Physics prize **alone**, so options listing co-laureates are wrong.
- Gaganyaan uses **LVM3 (HLVM3)**, not PSLV or GSLV Mk II. As of October 2026, G1 has **not yet flown**.
- PSLV-C62 **failed**. Some early headlines called it a success, so don't be fooled by "successfully placed".
- Artemis II **did not land** on the Moon.
- Neutrinos are **neutral**. Any option calling them "charged" or "strongly interacting" is wrong.
- Shubhanshu Shukla is the **first Indian on the ISS** and the **second Indian in space** (after Rakesh Sharma, 1984).
- DRDO's 2026 scramjet tests were **ground tests** (combustor), not flight tests.
