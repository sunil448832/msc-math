## 5. Memory hooks
1. **Analogy, G20 boycott:** It's like a family wedding where one powerful uncle (the US) skips it, but the family still signs the register (the declaration) without him.
2. **Story, January 2026:** The EU leaders came for the parade on 26 January and stayed for the "wedding" on 27 January: the FTA, the "mother of all deals".
3. **Number trick, 99 vs 91:** Khalilur Rahman won by 99–91. "Out of 190 votes cast, he won by 8."
4. **Mnemonic, the AI summit trail:** "**B**ritish **S**coundrels **P**ay **D**ues" = Bletchley 2023 → Seoul 2024 → Paris 2025 → Delhi 2026.
5. **Analogy, COP31 split:** Türkiye owns the stadium and hands out the trophy, while Australia is the referee running the match.
6. **GHI publisher:** "**C**oncern **W**ith **H**unger" = **C**oncern Worldwide + **W**elthunger**h**ilfe. It is NOT FAO.
7. **Number trick, India's ranks:** "**38**-**100**-**130**" (GII-GHI-HDI). Innovation is closest to the top, hunger in the middle, HDI furthest down.
8. **BRICS cycle:** "**R**ussia (Kazan 2024) → **B**razil (Rio 2025) → **I**ndia (Delhi 2026)." Think of a relay baton passing R → B → I.
9. **UNSG Article 97:** "The Council **nominates**, the Assembly **appoints**, like a selection committee shortlisting and the board confirming."
10. **El-Enany = first Arab UNESCO chief:** Picture the Pyramids sitting on UNESCO's Paris rooftop.

## 7. Exam traps
- "First G20 in Africa" refers to the **summit** (2025). The African Union had already joined as a member in 2023, in New Delhi.
- The India–EU FTA was **concluded/announced** in January 2026. Do not mark "in force" as correct.
- The UNSG is **not** elected by the Security Council alone. The GA appoints the candidate the SC recommends.
- In GHI questions, the trap is the publisher (FAO/WFP options are wrong).
- No Quad Leaders' Summit was held in India in 2026. Only the foreign ministers met in Delhi (May 2026).
- India's GII rank in 2026 is the **same** (38) as in 2025. Watch for "improved" options.
