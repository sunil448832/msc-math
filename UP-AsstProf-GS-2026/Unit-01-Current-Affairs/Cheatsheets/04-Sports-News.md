## 3. Memory hooks
1. **Analogy, T20 WC 2026:** India defended the crown like a family winning the same cooking contest twice in a row, in its own kitchen (Ahmedabad).
2. **Number trick:** "**3-96-255**": 3rd title, 96-run win, India's score of 255.
3. **Story, Spain's World Cup:** Spain's "Second Star": Rodri ran the midfield (Golden Ball) while Mbappé kept scoring (Golden Boot ×2), but Spain lifted the cup.
4. **Mnemonic for the 2026 multi-sport hosts, "G-A-M":** **G**lasgow (CWG), **A**ichi–Nagoya (Asian Games), **M**ilan–Cortina (Winter Olympics).
5. **Glasgow's "lean menu":** a restaurant cutting the menu from 20 dishes to 10. Hockey, cricket, wrestling, shooting and badminton were taken off, so India's boxers (7 golds) were the main course.
6. **Number trick, Asian Games:** "Hangzhou **107** → Aichi **85**": India's medal count fell by 22.
7. **Chess family link:** Vaishali is Praggnanandhaa's sister. The sister went to the Candidates final table and won it.
8. **Sindarov's double:** "**W**orld Cup in **W**inter (Nov 2025, Goa) → **C**andidates in **C**yprus (Apr 2026) → **G**ukesh in **G**eneva."
9. **UP angle:** Dhyan Chand was **born** in Prayagraj and **grew up** in Jhansi. "Born at the Sangam, raised by the Rani's fort."
10. **Women's WC 2025:** "**S**hafali **S**hone in the final, **D**eepti **D**ominated the tournament." Deepti Sharma is from Agra (UP).

## 5. Exam traps
- India's Asian Games 2026 total is **85**, not 107 (that was Hangzhou 2022). India finished 4th both at Glasgow and at Aichi–Nagoya.
- Glasgow 2026 had **no wrestling, shooting, hockey, cricket or badminton**, so any option crediting India's medals to these sports is wrong.
- Mbappé's Golden Boot in 2026 was his **second**, so "first player to win it twice" is correct.
- The Gukesh–Sindarov match is in **Geneva**, not Chennai (Gukesh's home city) or Singapore (the 2024 venue).
- "First women's World Cup for India" means the **ODI** World Cup. India have not yet won the women's T20 World Cup: the 2026 edition (Lord's, July 2026) went to Australia.
- Winter Olympics 2026 had **two** host cities, Milan and Cortina d'Ampezzo.
