## Solutions
**Q1 — (D).** **Spain** beat Argentina 1–0 after extra time at MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, New Jersey, on 19 July 2026 (July 2026). It was Spain's second title, after 2010. *Also remember:* Argentina won in 2022 (Qatar), and England beat France in the third-place match. The next World Cup (2030) is co-hosted by Spain, Portugal and Morocco, with centenary matches in South America.

**Q2 — (B).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct: this was the first 48-team World Cup and the first co-hosted by three nations. Statement 3 is wrong: **Kylian Mbappé** won the Golden Boot with 10 goals. He is the first player to win it twice (2022 and 2026). **Rodri** (Spain) won the Golden Ball (July 2026). *Also remember:* Golden Glove went to Unai Simón (Spain), and Best Young Player to Pau Cubarsí (Spain).

**Q3 — (C).** India scored 255/5 and bowled NZ out for 159 at the **Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad**. India and Sri Lanka co-hosted the tournament (March 2026). *Also remember:* India became the first team to win **three** T20 World Cups (2007, 2024, 2026) and the first to **defend** the title. Jasprit Bumrah (4/15) was Player of the Match in the final.

**Q4 — (C).** A is true: India won its first Women's World Cup on 2 November 2025 at DY Patil Stadium, Navi Mumbai (November 2025). R is false: the opponent was **South Africa**, beaten by 52 runs. *Also remember:* Shafali Verma (87 runs and 2 wickets) was Player of the Final, and **Deepti Sharma** (from Agra, UP) was Player of the Tournament. Harmanpreet Kaur was captain.

**Q5 — (A).** **Norway** topped Milano Cortina 2026 (6–22 February 2026) with 18 gold and 41 medals in total, ahead of the USA (February 2026). *Also remember:* India sent two athletes, Arif Khan (alpine skiing) and Stanzin Lundup (cross-country). India has never won a Winter Olympic medal. The 2030 Winter Olympics go to the French Alps.

**Q6 — (A).** Glasgow hosted CWG 2026 (23 July – 2 August 2026). Aichi–Nagoya (Japan) hosted the 20th Asian Games. Milan–Cortina d'Ampezzo (Italy) hosted the Winter Olympics. The FIFA final was at MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford. *Also remember:* **Ahmedabad** won the **2030 Centenary Commonwealth Games** in Glasgow on 26 November 2025. The first CWG was held in Hamilton, Canada, in 1930.

**Q7 — (D).** India finished **4th with 85 medals** (21 gold, 27 silver, 37 bronze). China topped the table, followed by Japan and South Korea (October 2026). *Also remember:* India's record is **107** medals at Hangzhou 2022 (28 gold). The men's hockey team beat Malaysia 5–1 to retain its title, and both cricket teams retained their golds (the men beat Pakistan by 19 runs, the women beat Sri Lanka by 147 runs). The next Asian Games are Doha 2030.

**Q8 — (A).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Sindarov, aged 19, won the World Cup in Goa in November 2025, beating Wei Yi, and was the youngest-ever World Cup winner. He then won the Candidates in Pegeia, Cyprus, unbeaten in April 2026. Vaishali won the Women's Candidates with 8.5/14 in April 2026 and will challenge Ju Wenjun. Statement 3 is wrong: the Gukesh–Sindarov match is in **Geneva**, Switzerland, in late November – December 2026 (status as of October 2026). *Also remember:* Divya Deshmukh won the FIDE Women's World Cup in July 2025. Vaishali is the sister of R. Praggnanandhaa.

**Q9 — (D).** The Davis Cup is the men's team competition in **tennis**. The table-tennis world team championships carry the **Swaythling Cup** (men) and the **Corbillon Cup** (women). *Also remember:* the Durand Cup (1888) is Asia's oldest football tournament. The Thomas Cup is men's badminton (India won in 2022), and the Uber Cup is women's badminton.

**Q10 — (B).** Dhyan Chand was born on 29 August 1905 in **Allahabad (Prayagraj)**. Jhansi is the trap: he grew up there, and it holds his memorial. *Also remember:* the highest sports honour is the **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna** (renamed in 2021). Lucknow has the **K.D. Singh Babu Stadium** and the **Ekana (BRSABV Ekana) Cricket Stadium**.

**Q11 — (B).** Women's ODI WC final (2 November 2025) → T20 WC final (8 March 2026) → FIFA WC final (19 July 2026) → Asian Games hockey final (early October 2026, during the Games of 19 September – 4 October 2026). *Also remember:* India beat Malaysia 5–1 in that final, retaining the hockey title it won at Hangzhou 2022.

**Q12 — (C).** **Boxing** gave India 10 medals (7 gold, 3 silver). India's total was 39 (13 gold, 17 silver, 9 bronze), behind Australia, England and Canada (August 2026). The trap is that **wrestling, shooting, hockey, cricket and badminton were not on the slimmed-down 10-sport Glasgow programme**. *Also remember:* for the first time in 24 years, India's women (8 gold) won more golds than the men (5).
