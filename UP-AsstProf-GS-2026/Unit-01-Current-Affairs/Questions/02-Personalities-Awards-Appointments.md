## Solutions
**Q1 — (C).** Justice Surya Kant, from Hisar (Haryana), succeeded B.R. Gavai and took oath (in Hindi) before President Murmu on 24 November 2025 (November 2025). He holds office until 9 February 2027, as of October 2026. *Also remember:* He was earlier Chief Justice of the Himachal Pradesh High Court (2018) and joined the Supreme Court in May 2019. A CJI retires at 65.

**Q2 — (A).** C.P. Radhakrishnan (NDA) defeated B. Sudershan Reddy (INDIA bloc) in the poll of 9 September 2025 and was sworn in as the **15th** Vice-President on 12 September 2025, after Jagdeep Dhankhar resigned in July 2025. He still holds the office as of October 2026 (his term runs until September 2030). *Also remember:* He is a two-time MP from Coimbatore and the third VP from Tamil Nadu, after S. Radhakrishnan and R. Venkataraman. Harivansh is the Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.

**Q3 — (D).** These are the October 2026 announcements. Physics (6 October): **Francis Halzen** (Belgian-born, University of Wisconsin) as sole laureate, for the IceCube Neutrino Observatory at the South Pole and the discovery of astrophysical high-energy neutrinos. Chemistry (7 October): **Kagan** (France) and **Soai** (Japan), for non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric synthesis (chirality). Medicine (5 October): **Deisseroth, Hegemann, Nagel**, for light-gated ion channels and **optogenetics**. Literature (8 October): Canadian poet-classicist **Anne Carson**. *Also remember:* Economics (12 October 2026) went to **Robert Hall and Michael Woodford** "for microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilisation". Each prize is worth SEK 12 million.

**Q4 — (B).** The Norwegian Nobel Committee announced on 9 October 2026 (October 2026) that Pillay was honoured "for her efforts to promote peace and international law". She is a Durban-born South African judge of Tamil descent. She was UN High Commissioner for Human Rights and earlier a judge of the ICTR and the ICC. *Also remember:* The Peace Prize is the only Nobel decided in **Oslo** (Norway). All the others are decided in Stockholm, Sweden.

**Q5 — (A).** The 2026 list (January 2026) had 5 Padma Vibhushan, 13 Padma Bhushan and 113 Padma Shri, for 131 in total. The Padma Vibhushan went to Dharmendra (posthumous), V.S. Achuthanandan (posthumous), K.T. Thomas, P. Narayanan and N. Rajam. Statement 3 is wrong because Dharmendra received the **Padma Vibhushan**, not the Padma Bhushan. *Also remember:* Mammootty and Alka Yagnik were among the Padma Bhushan awardees. N. Rajam, the Hindustani violinist, taught for decades at **BHU, Varanasi**, which is the UP link.

**Q6 — (D).** Bharatiya Jnanpith announced on 14 March 2026 (March 2026) that Tamil poet-lyricist R. Vairamuthu would receive the 60th Jnanpith. He received it in New Delhi in July 2026. He is only the third Tamil winner, after P.V. Akilan (1975) and D. Jayakanthan (2002). *Also remember:* The 59th Jnanpith (for 2024) went to Hindi writer **Vinod Kumar Shukla**, the first from Chhattisgarh. It was presented at his Raipur home on 21 November 2025, and he died in December 2025.

**Q7 — (B).** A is true: the Government announced on 16 September 2026 (September 2026) that Anant Nag would receive the Phalke Award for 2024, presented at the 72nd National Film Awards. R is also a true historical fact, but it does not explain why Nag was chosen. *Also remember:* Anant Nag is the second Kannada actor to get the award, after Dr. Rajkumar (1995). The previous award (for 2023) went to Malayalam actor **Mohanlal** (announced September 2025).

**Q8 — (C).** The Government named Lt Gen Dhiraj Seth, then Vice Chief, on 13 June 2026. He took over on 30 June 2026 (June 2026), succeeding General Upendra Dwivedi. *Also remember:* General Anil Chauhan is the Chief of Defence Staff (CDS), a different post. Don't confuse the CDS with the Army Chief (COAS).

**Q9 — (D).** The Hindi Sahitya Akademi Award 2025 (announced 16 March 2026) went to **Mamta Kalia** for her memoir *Jeete Jee Allahabad*, which is also a UP connection. Vinod Kumar Shukla is linked to the **Jnanpith**, not this award. The other pairs are correct. Szalay's *Flesh* won the Booker on 10 November 2025, beating Kiran Desai. *Taiwan Travelogue* won on 19 May 2026, the first winner translated from Mandarin. Faltings won the Abel in March 2026, for work on the Mordell and Lang conjectures. *Also remember:* The 2026 Booker Prize winner is due on 9 November 2026. Its shortlist includes Marlon James, Douglas Stuart and Elizabeth Strout.

**Q10 — (A).** Surya Kant became CJI on 24 November 2025, Raj Kumar Goyal became CIC on 15 December 2025, Nitin Nabin became BJP President on 20 January 2026, and Dhiraj Seth became COAS on 30 June 2026. *Also remember:* Nitin Nabin, a five-time Bihar MLA aged 45, is the BJP's youngest national president and succeeded J.P. Nadda. Goyal's appointment brought the Central Information Commission to its full 11-member strength.

**Q11 — (C).** Anandiben Patel became UP Governor in July 2019 and became the state's longest-serving Governor on 29 July 2025. She was still Governor in 2026: she addressed the UP Legislature's Budget Session in February 2026. *Also remember:* She was earlier the first woman Chief Minister of Gujarat (2014–16) and Governor of Madhya Pradesh. Ram Naik was her predecessor in Lucknow.

**Q12 — (B).** The 68th Ramon Magsaysay Awards (2026) went to **Tommy Koh** (Singapore, diplomat and international law), **Runa Khan** (Bangladesh, social entrepreneur) and **Bo Kyi** (Myanmar, human-rights defender and former political prisoner). They were announced on 31 August 2026 (August 2026), and the ceremony is scheduled for 15 November 2026 in Manila. *Also remember:* No Indian was among the 2026 awardees. The award is often called "Asia's Nobel".
