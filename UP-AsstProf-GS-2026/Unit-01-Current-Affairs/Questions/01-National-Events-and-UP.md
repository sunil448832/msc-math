## Solutions
**Q1 — (C).** PM Modi inaugurated the 594-km, 6-lane (expandable to 8) Ganga Expressway in Hardoi district on 29 April 2026 (April 2026). It runs from Meerut to Prayagraj through 12 districts and is UP's longest expressway. *Also remember:* At the inauguration, an extension from Meerut to Haridwar was announced. Lucknow–Ghazipur is the Purvanchal Expressway, Agra–Lucknow is its own expressway, and Chitrakoot–Etawah is the Bundelkhand Expressway.

**Q2 — (D).** The Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) [VB-G RAM G] Act, 2025 raises the guarantee from 100 to 125 days. Parliament passed it and it received the President's assent in December 2025. It came into force and repealed MGNREGA from 1 July 2026. *Also remember:* The Centre–State cost-sharing is 90:10 for 11 specified (North-Eastern/Himalayan) States and 60:40 for the others. Opposition states objected to the extra burden this puts on states.

**Q3 — (B).** Phase I (Houselisting) began on 1 April 2026 (April 2026), with each State/UT doing its field work within a 30-day window during April–September 2026. Self-enumeration and the mobile app make it the first digital census. Statement 3 is wrong because population enumeration is scheduled for **February 2027**. *Also remember:* The February 2027 population phase will also enumerate caste, and President Droupadi Murmu was reported as the first citizen to self-enumerate.

**Q4 — (A).** West Bengal: Suvendu Adhikari, the first BJP CM of the state (9 May 2026). Tamil Nadu: C. Joseph Vijay of TVK, the first non-Dravidian-party government in about six decades (10 May 2026). Keralam: V.D. Satheesan of the Congress-led UDF (18 May 2026). Bihar: Nitish Kumar took oath for a record 10th time (20 November 2025). *Also remember:* The BJP retained Assam, and the NDA retained Puducherry, in the May 2026 results (counting on 4 May 2026).

**Q5 — (A).** The Union Cabinet approved the change in February 2026, Parliament passed the Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026 on 11–12 August 2026, and the new name took effect on 25 August 2026. Article 3 is exactly the provision used, so R explains A. *Also remember:* Under Article 4, such a law is not a constitutional amendment for the purposes of Article 368, so a simple majority is enough. The State Legislature's views are not binding on Parliament.

**Q6 — (B).** The ₹230-crore, 65-acre Rashtra Prerna Sthal on the banks of the Gomti in Vasant Kunj, Lucknow, was inaugurated by the PM on 25 December 2025 (December 2025), Vajpayee's birth anniversary. It has 65-ft bronze statues of Vajpayee, Syama Prasad Mookerjee and Deendayal Upadhyaya, plus a lotus-shaped museum. *Also remember:* It was built on a former garbage dump, and the main UP Diwas celebration (24–26 January 2026) was held there on the theme "Viksit Bharat – Viksit Uttar Pradesh".

**Q7 — (C).** Dharma Dhwaja on the Ram temple: 25 November 2025. Union Budget: 1 February 2026. Ganga Expressway: 29 April 2026. Jewar commercial flights: 15 June 2026. So the order is 4-2-1-3. *Also remember:* The PM formally inaugurated Noida International Airport on 28 March 2026, but scheduled flights began only on 15 June 2026, with an IndiGo flight from Lucknow. Don't confuse the inauguration date with the start of operations.

**Q8 — (D).** The fiscal deficit for 2026-27 is pegged at 4.3% of GDP, down from the 4.4% estimate for 2025-26 (February 2026). *Also remember:* The total budget size was about ₹53.5 lakh crore, income-tax rates were unchanged, and the new Income-tax Act, 2025 takes effect from 1 April 2026.

**Q9 — (A).** The Code on Wages 2019, the Industrial Relations Code 2020, the Code on Social Security 2020 and the OSH & Working Conditions Code 2020 were all notified to take effect from 21 November 2025 (November 2025). Together they subsume 29 central laws. *Also remember:* The central rules came later: draft rules were published in December 2025, and final rules followed in 2026.

**Q10 — (B).** On 7 October 2026 (October 2026), the MPC under Governor Sanjay Malhotra unanimously raised the repo rate from 5.25% to 5.50%. This was the first hike since February 2023, and the stance changed from neutral to "calibrated tightening" because of inflation risks. *Also remember:* After the hike, the SDF is 5.25% and the MSF/Bank Rate is 5.75%, and the RBI projected FY27 growth at 7.1%.

**Q11 — (C).** India ranked 38th of 139 economies in GII 2026 (19th edition, released around September–October 2026), the same rank as in 2025. It is first among lower-middle-income economies and first in Central & Southern Asia. *Also remember:* Switzerland, Sweden and the USA are the top three, and Bengaluru and Delhi are India's top clusters.

**Q12 — (D).** The record ₹9,12,696 crore (≈ ₹9.12 lakh crore) UP budget was tabled on 11 February 2026 (February 2026). It is UP's largest budget so far, presented ahead of the 2027 Assembly polls. *Also remember:* A supplementary budget of ₹59,019 crore followed in August 2026. The previous year's (2025-26) budget was ≈ ₹8.08 lakh crore.
