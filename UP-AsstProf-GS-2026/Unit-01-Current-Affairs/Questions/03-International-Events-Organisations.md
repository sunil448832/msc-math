## Solutions
**Q1 — (C).** The G20 Leaders' Summit was held in Johannesburg on 22–23 November 2025 under South Africa's presidency. It was the first on African soil, and it adopted a declaration even though the United States boycotted the summit (November 2025). *Also remember:* the **United States** holds the G20 presidency for **2026**. The African Union became a permanent G20 member at India's New Delhi summit in September 2023.

**Q2 — (A).** European Commission President **Ursula von der Leyen** and European Council President **António Costa** were the joint chief guests on 26 January 2026. It was the first time two top EU leaders attended together (January 2026). *Also remember:* Indonesia's President Prabowo Subianto was chief guest in 2025, and France's Emmanuel Macron in 2024.

**Q3 — (B).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. The deal was announced at the 16th India–EU Summit at Hyderabad House, New Delhi, on 27 January 2026, and Ursula von der Leyen called it the "mother of all deals" (January 2026). Statement 3 is wrong: the deal still needs India's Cabinet approval and ratification by the European Parliament. *Also remember:* talks first began in 2007. The same summit adopted a 2030 roadmap and an EU–India Security and Defence Partnership. India signed its CETA with the UK in July 2025.

**Q4 — (D).** India took over the BRICS chair from **Brazil** (Rio summit, July 2025) on 1 January 2026. It hosted the 18th Summit at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 12–13 September 2026, which adopted the New Delhi Declaration (September 2026). *Also remember:* the 2024 summit was in Kazan (Russia) and the 2023 summit in Johannesburg (South Africa). India's earlier BRICS summits were in 2012, 2016 and 2021.

**Q5 — (C).** UNESCO – Khaled El-Enany (Egypt, in office from 15 November 2025). WTO – Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala (second term from 1 September 2025). IMF – Kristalina Georgieva. World Bank – Ajay Banga. All as of October 2026. *Also remember:* El-Enany is the **first UNESCO DG from an Arab country**. He succeeded Audrey Azoulay. HQs: UNESCO in Paris, WTO in Geneva, IMF and World Bank in Washington DC.

**Q6 — (D).** **Khalilur Rahman**, Bangladesh's Foreign Minister, was elected on 2 June 2026 by 99 votes to 91 over Cyprus's Andreas Kakouris. He took office in September 2026 (June 2026). *Also remember:* he succeeded **Annalena Baerbock** (Germany, 80th session). Philemon Yang (Cameroon) presided over the 79th session.

**Q7 — (C).** A is true: GHI 2026, released on 14 October 2026, ranked India **100th of 122** with a score of 24.1, which is "serious" (October 2026). R is false: the GHI is published by **Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe**, not by FAO. *Also remember:* GHI uses four indicators: undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting and under-5 mortality. India's child wasting (about 19%) was the worst in South Asia.

**Q8 — (A).** Bletchley Park, UK (November 2023) → Seoul (May 2024) → Paris AI Action Summit (February 2025) → New Delhi AI Impact Summit (February 2026). New Delhi was the first summit in the series hosted by a Global South country. *Also remember:* the New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact was adopted on 21 February 2026 at Bharat Mandapam and endorsed by about 88 countries and organisations. It is non-binding and follows the motto "Sarvajan Hitaya, Sarvajan Sukhaya" (February 2026).

**Q9 — (D).** The BRICS Summit 2025 was held in **Rio de Janeiro** (Brazil, July 2025). Kazan hosted the 2024 summit. The other pairs are correct: Johannesburg (November 2025), Belém, Brazil (COP30, November 2025), and Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan (26th SCO Summit, about 1 September 2026, the SCO's 25th anniversary year). *Also remember:* COP30 launched the **Tropical Forest Forever Facility (TFFF)**, and its main decision is known as the "Global Mutirão".

**Q10 — (A).** **Switzerland** topped GII 2026, followed by Sweden and the US. India was **38th of 139** economies, the same as in 2025, and first among lower-middle-income economies (October 2026). *Also remember:* GII is published by **WIPO** (Geneva). India was 81st in 2015, so its climb is a popular question.

**Q11 — (B).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct (Article 97). Statement 3 is wrong because the General Assembly *appoints* the SG after the Security Council *recommends* a candidate, and the P5 veto applies at the Council stage. The process began with a joint letter on 25 November 2025, and the new SG takes office on 1 January 2027 (status as of October 2026). *Also remember:* by informal practice the post rotates by region, and many observers expect a Latin American SG this time. Guterres is the 9th SG.

**Q12 — (B).** COP31 is in **Antalya, Türkiye**. Türkiye's Murat Kurum is COP31 President, and Australia's Chris Bowen is "President of Negotiations". The compromise was agreed at COP30 in Belém in November 2025 (status as of October 2026). *Also remember:* COP29 was in Baku (Azerbaijan) in 2024 and COP30 in Belém (Brazil) in 2025. Pre-COP events were planned in the Pacific.
