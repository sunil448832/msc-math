**31 — (B)** Statement 1 is correct: $255=3\cdot5\cdot17$ and $\gcd(255,\varphi(255))=\gcd(255,128)=1$, which is exactly the condition for every group of that order to be cyclic. Statement 2 is correct: $351=3^3\cdot13$ and $n_{13}\in\lbrace 1,27\rbrace$. If $n_{13}=27$, there are $27\cdot12=324$ elements of order $13$, and the remaining $27$ elements must form the unique Sylow 3-subgroup. Statement 3 is false: $GL_2(\mathbb F_3)$ has order $48$, with $n_3=4$ ($8$ elements of order $3$) and $n_2=3$ ($32$ elements of $2$-power order, more than $16$). So exactly two are correct.

**32 — (C)** Pair 1 is correct: an automorphism of $Q_8$ sends $i,j$ to any $x,y$ of order $4$ with $y\ne\pm x$, giving $6\cdot4=24$ automorphisms, and the group is the rotation group of the cube, $S_4$. Pair 2 is correct: there are $8$ automorphisms, the group is non-abelian, and it has exactly two elements of order $4$, so it is dihedral rather than $Q_8$. Pair 3 is correct: $\mathop{\mathrm{Aut}}\nolimits(\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_3)=GL_2(\mathbb F_3)$, of order $(9-1)(9-3)=48$. All three are matched.

**33 — (D)** $|GL_3(\mathbb F_3)|=26\cdot24\cdot18=2^5\cdot3^3\cdot13$, so a Sylow 3-subgroup has order $27$, and the upper unitriangular group $U$ is one.
